# Installment conversion rates

What it costs to pay in full and then ask the bank to re-split the charge, on the three issuers the household compares for this: First Choice (U PLAN), UOB (UOB PayLater) and ttb (`ttb so goood`). For how the converted rows are recorded and what they earn, see [[installment-reward-campaigns]].

Researched 2026-10-05 for a ฿17,988 China tour package over 10 months, where the travel agency charged no fee on UOB or ttb cards and a 2.3% card fee on everything else.

## The three products

| | First Choice U PLAN | UOB PayLater | ttb so goood |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rate | **0.39%/month flat** on the principal, 3–10 months; "เริ่มต้น 0.39%" (starting at), stated as 7.01%–15.83% a year reducing balance | **10.98%–16% a year, reducing balance**; the app quotes each cardholder's rate | **0.69%/month flat**, 6 or 10 months; 0% for 3 months on any slip |
| Rate is | **promotional** and may not be there next month or next year (user, 2026-10-05) | per cardholder | ttb's standard rate (0.59% for AIA premiums only, Sep–Dec 2026) |
| Minimum | slip ≥ ฿500, total ≥ ฿3,000 | ฿1,500 | slip ≥ ฿1,000 |
| Where | UCHOOSE, by 20:00 on the statement date | UOB TMRW or LINE Connect for unbilled charges; phone 02-123-0300 otherwise | ttb touch, 04:00–21:00 |
| Rewards | points lost; the original charge still counts toward NW4/IS4 (unconfirmed for plans with interest) | the original charge's points/cashback are taken back on approval, then **each term earns them instead**: "ผู้ขอเข้าร่วมโปรแกรมจะได้รับคะแนนสะสมหรือเครดิตเงินคืนตามยอดการชำระในแต่ละงวดแทน". On UOB One that's 1% per term (see [[../promotions/uob-one-2026#Installment rule]]) | none: `ttb so goood` spend is excluded from every ttb card's cashback, so which ttb card pays doesn't matter (user, 2026-10-05) |
| Source | [epr-0-3month](https://www.firstchoice.co.th/promotion/epr-0-3month); the household's 10-term plan at 0.39% (Nuta, `OMISE*ROOJAI`, 2026-10-03) | [uob-paylater](https://www.uob.co.th/personal/cards/credit/uob-paylater.page) | ttb so smart fact sheet v.07/67 (0.69% flat × 10 example); ttb's own so goood page lists only the 0% × 3 |

Flat and reducing-balance rates don't compare directly. Over 10 months, a flat monthly rate *f* costs *f* × 10 of the principal. UOB's 10.98% a year works out to the same money as about 0.51%/month flat, and 16% to about 0.75%.

## The 2026-10-05 comparison (฿17,988, 10 months)

| Option | Card fee | Interest | Total paid | Per month |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| UOB PayLater at 10.98% | ฿0 | ฿917.61 | **฿18,905.61** | ฿1,890.56 |
| First Choice U PLAN at 0.39% | ฿413.72 | ฿717.67 (on ฿18,401.72) | ฿19,119.39 | ฿1,911.94 |
| ttb so goood at 0.69% | ฿0 | ฿1,241.17 | ฿19,229.17 | ฿1,922.92 |
| UOB PayLater at 16% | ฿0 | ฿1,345.32 | ฿19,333.32 | ฿1,933.33 |
| First Choice at 0.69% (if the promo ends) | ฿413.72 | ฿1,269.72 | ฿19,671.44 | ฿1,967.14 |

The card fee is charged on the slip, so First Choice's interest runs on the fee too.

Break-even points:

- **UOB beats First Choice (at 0.39%) when UOB quotes up to 13.5% a year.**
- **First Choice loses to ttb above 0.45%/month.** The ฿413.72 fee uses up most of the gap between 0.39% and 0.69%.
- First Choice beats UOB at 10.98% only below 0.27%/month.

### Paid on UOB One (net of its 1%)

The user may pay on UOB One for the 1% (user, 2026-10-05). Each term earns 1%, rounded per line: ฿179.90 if UOB counts only the principal part of each term, up to ฿189.10 if it counts the whole term including interest (at 10.98%). Ten terms of about ฿19 each sit well inside the ฿2,000-a-cycle cap.

| UOB's quoted rate | Total paid | Net of the 1% |
|---|--:|--:|
| 10.98% | ฿18,905.61 | ฿18,716.51 – 18,725.71 |
| 13% | ฿19,077.10 | ฿18,886.30 – 18,897.20 |
| 15% | ฿19,247.71 | ฿19,055.21 – 19,067.81 |
| 16% | ฿19,333.32 | ฿19,140.02 – 19,153.42 |

On UOB One, **UOB beats First Choice (0.39%) up to about 15.6% a year** and beats ttb at every rate in its range. Only a 16% quote loses, and only to First Choice, by ฿21–34.

The merchant has to be a Thai one: a tour agency registered abroad that bills in THB would earn nothing (foreign-in-THB, [[../promotions/uob-one-2026#Exclusions]]).

### First Choice with TR3

A tour agency is out of NW4 and probably in First Choice's travel campaign, TR3 ([[../promotions/first-choice-2026h2#TR3 — travel (read 2026-10-05, not tracked)]]), if the purchase is made by 31 Oct 2026 with `TR3` registered. The ฿18,401.72 slip alone is one whole ฿10,000, so ฿150, which brings First Choice to ฿18,969.39. With ฿1,598.28 more First Choice travel spend that month it reaches ฿20,000, so ฿300 (฿18,819.39). UOB One then wins up to about 13.9% a year (฿150) or 12.1% (฿300), instead of 15.6%. All of this assumes a U PLAN plan with interest still counts toward TR3, which is unconfirmed.
