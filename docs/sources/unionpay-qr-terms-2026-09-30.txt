payment.
Participating Wallets: KTC Mobile, ICBC Mobile Banking, BOC TH Mobile Banking
How to register for the promotion:
1. Click https://marketing.unionpayintl.com/offer-promote/?language=en&insCode=299990156#/offer?offerNo=260722112617
2. Click “Register”
3. Log in or click “Create and account” to register
4. Click “Add UnionPay Card”

6. Click “Done”
7. Select the card you want to register for the promotion
8. Registered successful, then click “Understood”

4. The amount shown on the receipt or banking app may reflect the pre-discount amount. The actual payment deducted will be based on the final amount charged by the issuing bank.
