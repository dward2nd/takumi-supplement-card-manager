# AEON cashback — Rabbit 5%, World Mastercard 5% supermarkets, Everyday with AEON (NTW1), UnionPay 3%

Three AEON cashback streams, each tracked in the [[../concepts/promotion-bureau|Promotion Bureau]] **per statement cycle**. AEON's cycle runs from the 11th to the 10th, and the Bureau row's month is the month of the closing date, so `2026M9` means the cycle billed on 10 Sep. Rows join a period by their `Bill Cycle Date`. The Bureau row shows the period as starting on the previous closing date and ending the day before this one; that's a Bureau-wide convention, a day off AEON's own 11th–10th. Wired in 2026-09-29 (user), from the cycle billed 10 Sep 2026.

> **Structured source of truth**: `scripts/python/lib/bureau/aeon_rabbit.py`, `aeon_world.py`, `ntw1.py`, `aeon_unionpay.py`.

## Readings

- **NTW1 counts one card.** AEON allows one registration per national ID. Takumi registered AEON World Mastercard, so spend on AEON's other cards doesn't count (user, 2026-09-29). Baiboon's supplement spend on it (the `[บัตรหลัก]` rows) does.
- **AEON World and NTW1 both count the same supermarket spend.** The card's page says its cashback can't combine with other promotions. The household tracks both anyway, as Baiboon's trackers did before the Bureau.
- **AEON UnionPay is read off the Note.** Nuta's Alipay rows carry `% cb` 3% by hand and give the currency in the Note (`Original charge 600 CNY.`); the class reads it from there or from a `CHN`/`HKG`/`MAC`/`TWN` merchant suffix. It marks `% cb` 3% inside the ฿2,500 cap.
- **Only AEON Rabbit and UnionPay mark `% cb`.** Its rows carry 5% from `promotions/aeon-rabbit-cashback.yaml`. The Bureau adds the ฿50-per-transaction and ฿1,000-per-cycle caps the YAML could only note. AEON World and NTW1 rows keep `% cb` unset, as the household had them, so the money lives in the Bureau shares and the trackers.
- **Restaurants aren't linked automatically for NTW1.** MCC 5812 can't be read off a merchant string. A restaurant row linked by hand counts, because a ladder counts every linked row.
- **AEON World Mastercard does earn cashback.** The card's page lists the 5% supermarket benefit, so the old "no cashback" note on the card was wrong.
