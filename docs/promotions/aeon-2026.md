# AEON cashback — Rabbit 5%, World Mastercard 5% supermarkets, Everyday with AEON (NTW1), UnionPay 3%

Three AEON cashback streams, each tracked in the [[../concepts/promotion-bureau|Promotion Bureau]] **per statement cycle**. AEON's cycle runs from the 11th to the 10th, and the Bureau row's month is the month of the closing date, so `2026M9` means the cycle billed on 10 Sep. All four count by **transaction date**, 11th – 10th, whatever statement a row bills on (`AEONTransactionDated`, see *Readings*): NTW1 since 2026-10-24, AEON Rabbit, AEON World and AEON UnionPay since 2026-10-29 (user: "rename campaigns from AEON UnionPay and AEON World Mastercard too, since I got some misalignment from those cards as well"; see *Credits against the two countings*). Their Bureau rows carry AEON's own dates, not the Bureau-wide cycle convention (previous closing date to the day before this one): `2026M10` runs 11 Sep – 10 Oct, and the tracker reads `11 Sep—10 Oct` (user, 2026-10-29). Wired in 2026-09-29 (user), from the cycle billed 10 Sep 2026.

> **Structured source of truth**: `scripts/python/lib/bureau/aeon_rabbit.py`, `aeon_world.py`, `ntw1.py`, `aeon_unionpay.py`.

## Readings

- **NTW1 counts one card.** AEON allows one registration per national ID. Takumi registered AEON World Mastercard, so spend on AEON's other cards doesn't count (user, 2026-09-29). Baiboon's supplement spend on it (the `[บัตรหลัก]` rows) does.
- **NTW1 counts by transaction date, strictly 11th–10th** (AEON's call centre, 2026-10-24). A row counts in the period it was made in, whatever statement it bills on. AEON posts purchases one to four days late, so a purchase dated the 9th or 10th often bills a cycle later but still counts in the earlier period. A cancelled order and its refund in the same period cancel out there. `AEONTransactionDated` overrides `period_for`, `covers` and `candidate_filter` for this, and `billed_on`: its Bureau rows end on the closing date itself, so they keep their names (`2026M10`) and tracker dates (10 Oct) but run 11th – 10th (2026-10-29). See *NTW1 by transaction date* below.
- **AEON Rabbit's 5% counts by transaction date too** (2026-10-28; switched 2026-10-29, user: "update the promotion bureau to work that way"). The 2 Oct credit was ฿100.25, ฿0.50 under the Bureau's ฿100.75 for the cycle billed 10 Sep. By statement cycle that's ฿2,015 of LINE Pay and Rabbit top-ups. By transaction date 11 Aug–10 Sep it's ฿2,005: the two slips dated 10 Aug (฿10 + ฿45) count in the period before, and the ฿45 dated 10 Sep, billed on 10 Oct, counts here. 5% of ฿2,005 is ฿100.25 to the satang. `AEONRabbitCashback` now uses `AEONTransactionDated` (`lib/bureau/aeon_cycle.py`), the mixin NTW1's rule was moved into. The switch relinked three periods. The two 10 Aug slips came off `2026M9` and stay unlinked: no Bureau row exists for the period ending 10 Aug, and the 2 Sep credit (฿75.96) paid them. The 10 Sep ฿45 moved to `2026M9`. The two 9 Oct top-ups (฿300 + ฿250, billed 10 Nov) moved to `2026M10`, which went from ฿46.00 to ฿71.25. The credit due about 2 Nov tests it.
- **Credits come in later cycles.** AEON usually pays on the 2nd after the statement, but its terms allow up to 90 days (World 5%, UnionPay) or 120 days (NTW1); see *When AEON must pay* below. World's 5% for 11 Jul–10 Aug (฿500) came on 2 Oct, the bill after the one with NTW1's ฿120 alone, as AEON's call centre confirmed (user, 2026-10-29). That was a cycle later than usual, but inside the 90 days.
- **AEON World and NTW1 both count the same supermarket spend.** The card's page says its cashback can't combine with other promotions. The household tracks both anyway, as Baiboon's trackers did before the Bureau.
- **AEON UnionPay is read off the Note.** Nuta's Alipay rows carry `% cb` 3% by hand and give the currency in the Note (`Original charge 600 CNY.`); the class reads it from there or from a `CHN`/`HKG`/`MAC`/`TWN` merchant suffix. It marks `% cb` 3% inside the ฿2,500 cap.
- **UnionPay refunds come off the charge.** A CNY refund (`UNIONPAY MERCHANT BEIJING CHN` −฿1,128.62, Note `Original refund -225 CNY …`) is linked and netted off Nuta's charge of the same amount, so a refunded charge earns nothing ([[../concepts/promotion-bureau#Refunds come off the charge they give back|refunds]], 2026-10-02). The refund row carries 3% too, so the ledger's `cashback` nets to zero.
- **Only AEON Rabbit and UnionPay mark `% cb`.** Its rows carry 5% from `promotions/aeon-rabbit-cashback.yaml`. The Bureau adds the ฿50-per-transaction and ฿1,000-per-cycle caps the YAML could only note. AEON World and NTW1 rows keep `% cb` unset, as the household had them, so the money lives in the Bureau shares and the trackers.
- **GO Wholesale counts for NTW1** as a supermarket; it posts as `CFW-<branch> …` (Central Food Wholesale), matched since 2026-09-30.
- **Restaurants aren't linked automatically for NTW1.** MCC 5812 can't be read off a merchant string. A restaurant row linked by hand counts, because a ladder counts every linked row.
- **AEON World Mastercard does earn cashback.** The card's page lists the 5% supermarket benefit, so the old "no cashback" note on the card was wrong.

### When AEON must pay (terms read 2026-10-29)

AEON is often late with cashback (user, 2026-10-29: "This is not the first time AEON is late for their cashback"). Its terms allow for that. Each card's page and NTW1's page give a deadline, and a window to report a credit that hasn't come. Read on 2026-10-29, in Thai only:

| Stream | Deadline (verbatim) | Missing credit: report |
|---|---|---|
| AEON World 5% ([card page](https://www.aeon.co.th/aeon/cards/aeon-world-mastercard)) | `ภายใน 90 วันนับจากวันสรุปยอดบัญชีบัตรเครดิต`: within 90 days of the statement date (the 10th) | within 30 days after that |
| AEON UnionPay 3% ([card page](https://www.aeon.co.th/aeon/cards/aeon-unionpay-platinum-card)) | `ภายใน 90 วันนับจากวันสรุปยอดบัญชีบัตรเครดิต (ทุกวันที่ 10 ของเดือน)` | within 30 days after that; a wrong credit within 30 days of receiving it |
| AEON Rabbit 5% / 1% ([card page](https://www.aeon.co.th/aeon/cards/aeon-rabbit-platinum-card)) | `ภายใน 2 รอบบัญชี นับตั้งแต่รอบบัญชีที่มียอดใช้จ่าย`: within two statement cycles, counted from the cycle of the spend | within 30 days after that; a wrong credit within 90 days of receiving it |
| NTW1 ([promotion page](https://www.aeon.co.th/aeon/promotions/happy-monthly-with-aeon-2026)) | `ภายใน 120 วันนับถัดจากวันสิ้นสุดของแต่ละรอบบัญชี`: within 120 days after each period ends | `ภายใน 30 วันนับแต่พ้นกำหนดการได้รับเงินคืน`, with evidence, for a missing or wrong credit |

No AEON page says 60 days. Baiboon's open and recent periods against those deadlines:

| Period | World 5% (฿500) | NTW1 |
|---|---|---|
| 11 Jul – 10 Aug | paid 2 Oct (due by 8 Nov) | ฿120 paid 2 Sep (due by 8 Dec) |
| 11 Aug – 10 Sep | **not paid; due by 9 Dec**, report by 8 Jan 2027 | ฿120 paid 2 Oct (due by 8 Jan 2027) |
| 11 Sep – 10 Oct | due by 8 Jan 2027 | ฿340 due by 7 Feb 2027 |

So a 5% that misses the usual 2nd isn't missing yet. Leave its tracker open, without chasing AEON, until the deadline passes. Then the report window opens: 30 days, with the Makro PRO orders as evidence. UnionPay's May 2026 credit (on the 10 Jul statement) was inside its 90 days too.
