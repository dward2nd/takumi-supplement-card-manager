# ttb so smart — the card's 1%, and the Caltex, Bangchak, hypermarket, MUJI and UNIQLO campaigns

ttb so smart is one card account: Takumi's primary (…7368) and Baiboon's supplement (…0864) pool into the primary cardholder's quota for everything below. Tracked in the [[../concepts/promotion-bureau|Promotion Bureau]] from September 2026. The hypermarket campaign is the exception: it has one quota for the whole campaign, so its row runs from 1 Jul (user, 2026-09-29).

> **Structured source of truth**: `scripts/python/lib/bureau/ttb_so_smart.py` and `ttb_campaigns.py`.

| Stream | Class | Period | Pays | Cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1% cashback** (card benefit) | `TTBSoSmartCashback` (credit cap) | standing | 1% on every card purchase not excluded, paid into a ttb no fixed savings account | ฿2,000/statement cycle per card account |
| **CTG**, Caltex fuel | `TTBCaltexPromotion` (per slip) | 1 Jul – 31 Dec 2026 | 3% of a ฿600+ slip, at most ฿18, so ฿18 a slip | 4 slips (฿72)/month per person |
| **BCG**, Bangchak fuel | `TTBBangchakPromotion` (per slip) | 1 Jul – 31 Dec 2026 | the same, at Bangchak | ฿72/month per person |
| **BMG**, hypermarkets | `TTBHypermarketPromotion` (per slip) | 1 Jul – 30 Sep 2026 | per slip at Big C, Go Wholesale, Big C Online, Makro PRO: ฿50 (฿3k–4,999), ฿100 (฿5k–19,999), ฿450 (฿20k–99,999), ฿1,500 (฿100k+) | ฿1,500 per person for the campaign |

## Readings

- **The 1%'s exclusions** (bank's list, from the user):
  - utilities (MCC 4900, from 15 Jan 2026);
  - insurance of every kind, and unit-linked;
  - funds;
  - cash advances and transfers;
  - cancelled charges;
  - interest, fees and penalties;
  - ttb so goood, pay plan and cash chill chill installments;
  - foreign exchange;
  - business spend;
  - taxes;
  - merchants registered in China or the EEA/UK, in any currency;
  - 7-Eleven and TrueMoney Wallet;
  - foreign-registered merchants charging in baht;
  - petrol stations;
  - public transport.

  The rate still comes from `promotions/ttb-so-smart-cashback.yaml`, and the Bureau adds the cap. Rows keep `% cb` 1% inside the cap; the row the cap runs out on, and everything past it, are left unset.
- **Fuel pays a flat ฿18.** 3% × ฿600 = ฿18, which is the per-slip cap, so every qualifying slip pays ฿18. The 5% (฿30) tier needs a ttb car or home loan. Baiboon's `ttb Bangchak 3% Aug 2026` ฿54 is 3 × ฿18.
- **The hypermarket slips add up.** The page's boilerplate speaks of "the highest slip per round". Baiboon's own tracker, `Hyper 1Jul—30Sep` ฿500, reads each slip as earning its own tier, up to ฿1,500, and the Bureau reproduces it: five Makro PRO slips of ฿10,284–19,875, at ฿100 each. Only Makro PRO online counts, not Makro in store.
- **GO Wholesale posts as `CFW-<branch> …`** (Central Food Wholesale); the hypermarket class matches it since 2026-09-30.
- **Fuel and hypermarket rows never carry `% cb`.** Fuel earns no 1%, and a fixed ฿18 or ฿100 isn't a rate.
- **The statement prints the 1% ttb paid.** Under the balance box are three figures. The PDF text runs them as `0.00 398.31 906.51`: cash deposited to a No Fixed account, `เครดิตเงินคืนรอบนี้` / This Cycle Cash Back Received, then `เครดิตเงินคืนรวมปีนี้` / This Year Cash Back Received. The order is confirmed by the September statement (`0.00 367.14 508.20`), since 508.20 + 398.31 = 906.51. "This cycle" is the 1% for the cycle **before**, paid into Takumi's savings account between the two statements. So the next statement confirms a ttb so smart tracker, not just Takumi's word ([[../databases/cashback-trackers#How a row is settled|trackers]]). Checked on 2026-10-29:

  | Cycle earned | Trackers | ttb paid (statement) |
  |---|---|---|
  | 27 Jul – 26 Aug | Baiboon ฿367.14 | ฿367.14 (27 Sep statement) |
  | 27 Aug – 26 Sep | Baiboon ฿397.50 + Takumi ฿0.82 = ฿398.32 | ฿398.31 (27 Oct statement), a satang short |
  | 27 Sep – 26 Oct (`2026M10`) | Takumi ฿35.24 + Baiboon ฿7.83 = ฿43.07 | due on the 27 Nov statement |
- **No 1% at Makro from 3 Nov 2026.** The 27 Oct statement's notice: `ตั้งแต่วันที่ 3 พฤศจิกายน 2569 เป็นต้นไป การใช้จ่ายที่ Makro ผ่านบัตรเครดิต ttb ทุกประเภท จะไม่นำมาคำนวณในการสะสมคะแนน สำหรับบัตรเครดิตประเภทที่สะสมคะแนน หรือการรับเงินคืน 1% สำหรับบัตรเครดิต ttb so smart`. From 3 Nov, Makro spend on any ttb card earns no points, and none of ttb so smart's 1%. Other spend earns as before. The notice doesn't say whether Makro PRO counts. Not yet in `ttb-so-smart-cashback.yaml` or `TTBSoSmartCashback` (2026-10-29).

## From October 2026 (read 2026-10-01)

> **Structured source of truth**: `ttb_shopping.py` (MUJC, BGO) and `uniqlo.py` (UQCB, see [[uniqlo-2026]]).

| Stream | Class | Period | Pays | Cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **BGO**, Big C / GO Wholesale (BMG's successor) | `TTBBigCGoPromotion` (per slip) | 1 Oct – 31 Dec 2026 | the same ฿50 / 100 / 450 / 1,500 tiers from ฿3,000 / 5,000 / 20,000 / 100,000 a slip | ฿1,500 per person for the campaign |
| **MUJC**, MUJI | `TTBMujiPromotion` (per slip) | 1 Oct – 31 Dec 2026 | ฿40 per whole ฿1,200 (at most ฿160); ฿200 at ฿6,000–9,999; ฿500 from ฿10,000 | ฿1,500 per person for the campaign |
| **UQCB**, UNIQLO | `TTBUniqloPromotion` (per slip) | 1 Oct 2026 – 28 Feb 2027 | ฿150 per whole ฿3,000 (at most ฿450); ฿800 from ฿12,000 | ฿800/month per person |

- **BGO drops Makro PRO.** Only Big C and GO Wholesale count, in store and online (GO Wholesale posts as `CFW-…`). Baiboon's Makro PRO slips, which filled BMG, earn nothing here. E-wallet payments are excluded.
- **BGO and MUJC cap the whole campaign**, so each has one Bureau row from its first day (`2026M10`, 1 Oct – 31 Dec), as BMG did. Slips are read as adding up to the cap, as for BMG.
- **The points halves don't apply.** MUJI 10%, UNIQLO 12% and **Joy of Shopping** (10% at participating malls all of 2026, `JOYP`) redeem ttb rewards plus points, and ttb so smart earns none. Joy of Shopping isn't modelled.
