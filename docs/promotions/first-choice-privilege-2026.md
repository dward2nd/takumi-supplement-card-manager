# Krungsri First Choice Privileges 2026 — BLUE PLUS

A status tier, not a cashback campaign. First Choice (Takumi's primary …9214, Baiboon's …1064, Nuta's …1285) reaches **BLUE PLUS** once the account's accumulated spend for calendar 2026 hits **฿400,000**. Not tracked in the [[../concepts/promotion-bureau|Promotion Bureau]]: there's no credit to split, only a threshold for the whole account.

Source: <https://www.firstchoice.co.th/first-choice-privilege>, read 2026-10-01.

## Terms

- **Accumulation**: `คำนวณยอดใช้จ่ายสะสม ตั้งแต่วันที่ 1 ม.ค. 69 - 31 ธ.ค. 69`, through the credit card, **primary and supplements together** (`ทั้งบัตรหลักและบัตรเสริม`), with no overdue payment in the past 12 months. No registration.
- **What counts**: `ยอดใช้จ่ายสะสม = ยอดใช้จ่ายแบบรูดเต็มจำนวน + ยอดผ่อนชำระสินค้า + ยอดกดเงินสดทุกช่องทาง`, i.e. full-amount spend, installment purchases and cash advances. Cash advances count only when they are ≥ ฿30,000 each, under the conditions the page sets.
- **Excluded** (`รายการยกเว้น`): cancelled transactions; card misuse; FX and crypto (MCC 4829, 5933, 6012, 6051, 7276, 7321); funds and investment-linked or unit-linked life insurance; investment units and brokers (MCC 6211); monthly or yearly auto-debit (MCC 0); "other" (MCC 9999). Utilities and telecom are **not** on the list.
- **Status rounds**: the bank checks spend at each month-end and announces status on the 25th–28th of the next month. Spend for 1 Jan – 30 Sep 2026 is round 8, announced 25–28 Oct, with privileges from 1 Nov. Status lasts to **31 Mar 2027**.
- **What it gives**: no annual fee; `BPCB` cashback (register on the 1st of each month; ≥ ฿300,000 in the month; ฿500 per ฿100,000, capped at ฿3,000 a month; first 200 members); points-to-cashback redemption at 18–20% in insurance, health & beauty and supermarket MCCs; e-coupons.

## Counting from the statements

**Read the condition verbatim** (user, 2026-10-01). Every charge counts unless the page's own `รายการยกเว้น` names it, and for this household that leaves only cancelled transactions (the other exclusions are the regulated categories: FX, crypto, funds, investments). A row that earns no points or cashback still counts, because the bank has its own reasons for withholding rewards. TrueMoney (`TMN…`), petrol, foreign merchants in THB and personal-loan-line installments are all spend.

- **Count the charge lines, never net of credits** (user: "count only spending without cashback in it"). `เครดิตเงินคืน NW2/NW3/TR…` lines aren't subtracted.
- **Plan On Demand counts once, as the original slip.** A slip converted to a 3-term plan shows up on three kinds of line: the purchase (`ICARE-CT-CHIANGMAI AIR` ฿3,000), its reversal (`REV-FC PLAN ON DEMAND: …` −฿3,000) and three `FIRST CHOICE PLAN ON DEMAND <balance> 00n/003` terms on the primary's section. Count the purchase line only. Drop the reversal and the terms. The reversal is **not** a cancellation: it only moves the slip into the installment terms (user, 2026-10-01), so the slip still counts once.
- **A merchant installment counts at the plan's full amount** (`ยอดผ่อนชำระสินค้า`), not term by term.
- **A refunded slip is a cancelled transaction.** `AMP*AIS ONLINE STORE` ฿34,300 (9 Aug, …1064) was credited back in full on 12 Aug, so neither line counts.
- **Payments and cashback rows are not spend.**
