# Campaign lineage — every campaign the catalogue research has met

The payment campaigns found while writing the [[../databases/promotion-catalogues|Promotion Catalogues]] pages, grouped by issuer. Each row gives a campaign's **predecessor → successor** chain, the pages it appears on, and where the household tracks it (a [[../promotions/uob-one-2026|promotion note]] or a [[../concepts/promotion-bureau|Promotion Bureau]] class). Banks renew most campaigns monthly or quarterly under a new code, so each month's research starts here:
- **continues**: check the end date;
- **ended**: look for the successor;
- **not published yet**: look again.

- **Pages**: **M** Makro · **G** GO Wholesale · **S** Shopee · **T** The 1 (Central The 1 in Sep) · **L** Lazada · **K** TikTok Shop. Lazada and TikTok Shop were Sep only. · **H** Sriphat Medical Center · **Y** Yunomori Onsen & Spa (both from Oct 2026) · **E** Thaiticketmajor (event tickets, from 3 Oct 2026) · **A** Apple Products (Apple and its resellers, from 3 Oct 2026) · **U** UNIQLO (from 5 Oct 2026).
