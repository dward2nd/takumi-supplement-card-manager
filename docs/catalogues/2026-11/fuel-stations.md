# Fuel stations — Shell and PTT, November 2026 (merchant side)

Researcher: `merchants-wallets`. Bank-card fuel campaigns (KTC, Krungsri PTT2 successor, ttb CTG/BCG, CardX, KBank…) are the bank researchers' job; this file covers what **Shell** and **OR** run themselves, plus the facts the plan needs (who runs the stations, how they take money, what the slips look like, prices). Labels: **V** verified on the official page today · **R** my reading of official terms · **T** third-party only · **U** unverified.

**How the household fills up** (ledgers, read-only, all rows to 1 Nov): small fills, ฿100–800 a slip, mostly ฿300–500. Baiboon (Chiang Mai): Shell on First Choice (8), KTC UnionPay (7), UOB World (6), UOB Premier (3), Krungsri JCB (1); PTT on First Choice, UOB Premier, UOB One. Nuta (Bang Saen): PTT on First Choice (32) and UOB One (1). Takumi: PTT on KTC Digital VISA. So almost every per-slip minimum of ฿1,000 in this file misses the household's normal fill; the offers that fit are per-litre discounts with no minimum, low-minimum stamps, and app points.

# 1. Shell (เชลล์)

## 1.1 Who runs the stations; descriptors

- Shell Thailand (บริษัท เชลล์แห่งประเทศไทย จำกัด) brands the stations. Its "ลงทุนกับเชลล์" pages describe two formats: **Company Owned (CO)** stations, run for Shell by a retailer company under the "Shell MBA Retailer" programme ("เป็นผู้ประกอบการสถานีบริการเชลล์ (Company Owned:CO)"; Shell funds the station and its running costs), and **dealer-built stations** ("investors-are-created-and-managed-service-stations"). https://www.shell.co.th/th_th/customer/service-stations/shell-investments/executive-service-stations.html · **V**.
- **Descriptor pattern** in the household's ledgers: `SHELL <4-digit site no.><letter> <CO|DO> <site/operator name> <city> TH`. Examples: `SHELL 0583F CO THONG T CHIANGMAI TH`, `SHELL 0583S CO THONG T…`, `SHELL 0589F CO THONG T…`, `SHELL 0892F CO SIRIWANI…`, `SHELL 1270F CO THONGPA LAMPHUN`, `SHELL 1271L CO THONGTH…`, `SHELL 2209F DO BILLION…`, `SHELL 2292F DO FORTY N…`, `SHELL 0732F CO RUNGROJ NONGKHAI`.
  - **CO / DO** most likely = **Company Owned / Dealer Owned** (Shell's own term "Company Owned:CO" above; `DO` sites carry a different operator name). **R**.
  - The letter after the site number splits one site into terminals: the same site 0583 posts as **F** and **S**. The amounts fit **F = forecourt (fuel)**, **S = shop (Shell Select / café)**; **L** (1271L) might be the lubricant bay (Helix Plus). **U** — no Shell source explains it.
  - Shell-wide bank campaigns sometimes show on statements as `CB-025 SHELL-CALTEX NOV 2025` (Baiboon's ledger) — a bank's cashback line, not a Shell code.
- **MCC**: not published by Shell; **not found**.
- The household's stations: **0583 (F and S) "CO THONG T…" and 0589F**, Chiang Mai; also 0892F, 2209F, 2292F, 1270F Lamphun, 1271L. All but 2209F/2292F are CO stations.

## 1.2 How Shell takes money

- **Cards**: the ledgers show Visa (First Choice), Mastercard (UOB World/Premier), JCB (Krungsri JCB) and **UnionPay (KTC UnionPay, 7 slips at Shell)** going through at CO stations. **V** (ledger evidence; Shell publishes no acceptance list).
- **Shell Card / Shell Card QR** — fleet cards for businesses only (call centre 02 657 9700). **V** (help page).
- **Shell Gift Card / Shell V-Power Gift Card** — a Bangkok Bank prepaid card, ฿300–50,000, valid 1 year, used at stations "ที่มีเครื่องรูดบัตร EDC"; ฿30 issue fee under ฿1,000. https://www.shell.co.th/th_th/customer/service-stations/shell-gift-card.html · **V**.
- **In-app payment (Shell Asia app)**: "Pay for fuels … using integrated digital payment — **Available in Malaysia & Singapore**", so **not in Thailand**. App Store listing id1539429518. **V**.
- **PromptPay QR, TrueMoney, ShopeePay, LINE Pay, UnionPay QR at Shell**: **not found** on any Shell page; TrueMoney's promotion list has no Shell (or PTT) entry on 1 Nov. Whether a given station shows a PromptPay QR is station-level; ask at 0583/0589.
- **Gov't 60/40 (ends 30 Nov)**: stations are juristic businesses; the scheme only admits non-juristic shops, ธงฟ้า shops, village-fund/community-enterprise shops and juristic persons with 2567 revenue ≤ ฿1.8M → **Shell/PTT forecourts don't qualify**. **R** (scheme rules, see [[merchants-wallets]] §4.3).

# 2. PTT Station (OR — บริษัท ปตท. น้ำมันและการค้าปลีก จำกัด (มหาชน))

## 2.1 Who runs the stations; descriptors

- PTT Station is OR's brand. OR recruits **dealers** ("ผู้แทนจำหน่ายสถานีบริการน้ำมัน PTT Station", https://pttstation.pttor.com/home) and runs company stations through its subsidiary **PTT Retail Management (PTTRM)**, which also runs Jiffy. **V** (OR site navigation; blueplus+ partner link `pttrm.com` for Jiffy).
- **Descriptor patterns** in the ledgers: `PTTST.C <station> <city>` (e.g. `PTTST.C CHIANG MAI UNIV…`, `PTTST.C MAEWANG…`, `PTTST.C BANGSAEN CHONBURI`), `PTTST.D <dealer name> <city>` (e.g. `PTTST.D DEEJAI SERVICE CHONBURI`, `PTTST.D TIDA MAHANAKOR…`, `PTTST.D SUKHUM SERVICE…`, `PTTST.D KORBCHAI PETROL…`, `PTTST.D A & S OIL…`), and `PTTRM_<province code> <station>` (`PTTRM_CM THAMMASART…`, `PTTRM_CB BANGPRA CHONBURI`).
  - `.D` rows carry a dealer's trading name (… SERVICE, … PETROL, … OIL), so **`.D` = dealer-operated**; `.C` rows carry a place name, so **`.C` = company (COCO) station**; `PTTRM_` = a station (or shop) billed by **PTT Retail Management** itself. **R** (pattern + OR's structure; no OR page defines the codes).
  - Café Amazon inside stations bills separately; the vault's UOB One rule matches `AMZ` and `RMAMZ` (Retail-Management Amazon) at **10%** (`scripts/repositories/promotions/uob-one-2026.yaml`) while UOB earns nothing on the fuel itself. Also `KFC3293-SPTT OR NA YAI…` = a KFC inside a PTT station.
- **MCC**: not published; **not found**.
- Household stations: Chiang Mai — `PTTST.C CHIANG MAI UNIV`, `PTTST.C MAEWANG`, `PTTRM_CM THAMMASART`; Chonburi — `PTTST.C BANGSAEN`, `PTTST.D DEEJAI SERVICE` (Nuta's most used, 15+ slips), `PTTST.D TIDA MAHANAKOR`, `PTTRM_CB BANGPRA`.
