## 2. Makro (stores) and Makro PRO

### 2.1 KTC

**A. Supermarket points → 13% (MKTHM-0549): continues to 31 Dec** · V
- Points equal to the slip (satang rounded up) → 13% back (฿0.13 a point). At most 30,000 points per cardholder per month, all stores together (฿3,900).
- **Makro in store only by scanning with the card in KTC Mobile, or by paying through TrueMoney Wallet.** Makro PRO online counts. Also GO Wholesale (store and online), Big C, Lotus's, Donki, Golden Place, Mitsukoshi Depachika, UFM Fuji Super; online Freshket.
- Register **every time, on the spend day**, at ktc.promo/supermarket2026 (or by QR); confirmation reply needed. Per card when several are registered.
- Credited within 60 days after each calendar month. Excludes ROP, Cash Back, Corporate, government cards.
- KTC UnionPay earns **no** normal points at MCC 5411, but this is a redemption, not earning — any KTC card with points can use it.
- https://www.ktc.co.th/promotion/shopping/supermarkets-convenience-stores/supermarket

**B. KTC × Makro appliance 0% installments (MOFCE-1073): continues to 31 Dec** · V (October; page lastmod 24 Sep)
- 0% up to 10 months in Makro's appliance department; installment purchases earn no KTC points. https://www.ktc.co.th/promotion/electronics-mobile/home-appliances/makro-installment

**C. KTC cards at Makro stores**: KTC's article (Jul 2026) says KTC cards can't yet be used to pay at Makro stores; the 13% terms agree (scan in KTC Mobile or TrueMoney only). Makro PRO takes KTC Visa/Mastercard/JCB/UnionPay. · V (October)

### 2.2 AEON

**A. NEW — AEON "แม็คโคร" (Makro in store), 11 Oct 2026 – 10 Apr 2027** · V
- **Mechanic**: each slip of **฿5,000 or more** at **any Makro branch**, paid by swiping an AEON credit card **or scanning a QR code** (`รูดชำระผ่านบัตรเครดิตอิออนหรือสแกน QR Code`) → **1.20%** back.
- **Caps**: ฿350 per statement cycle (reached at ≈ ฿29,167 of qualifying slips); ฿2,100 per national ID over the six cycles. **Bonus ฿1,400** once cumulative qualifying spend over the whole campaign reaches **฿300,000**. Total up to ฿3,500 ("รับเครดิตเงินคืนรวมสูงสุด 3,500 บาท").
- **Cycles** (AEON's 11th–10th): 11 Oct – 10 Nov, 11 Nov – 10 Dec, 11 Dec – 10 Jan, 11 Jan – 10 Feb, 11 Feb – 10 Mar, 11 Mar – 10 Apr.
- **Excluded**: online orders and every e-wallet (TrueMoney, LINE Pay, ShopeePay, เป๋าตัง…), Grab, LINE MAN, TikTok, Chat & Shop; cancellations, top-up/cash/gift cards, partial deposits. So **Makro PRO doesn't count** — Baiboon's AEON World spending is Makro PRO today.
- **Cards**: AEON World Mastercard, AEON Visa Platinum, Gold, Classic, Wellness Platinum, J-Premier Platinum, Petster, **AEON NextGen digital**. (AEON Rabbit, Primo and UnionPay are not listed.)
- **Registration**: once, in **AEON THAI MOBILE** or aeon.co.th, **before** spending; only spend from the registration day counts. **One principal card per national ID**; the last successful registration wins and cancels earlier ones; supplement spend counts toward the principal.
- Full payment only, in Thailand, in baht. A cycle whose spend exceeds twice the credit limit gets nothing.
- **Credited within 120 days after each cycle ends**; report a missing credit within 30 days after that deadline.
- Household: Takumi's AEON World (with Baiboon's `[บัตรหลัก]` spend) qualifies if registered and if Baiboon pays **in store** with slips ≥ ฿5,000. Registration is separate from NTW1's (also one card per ID); whether registering World here affects NTW1 isn't stated — the two are different campaigns with their own registrations. · R
- https://www.aeon.co.th/aeon/promotions/makro-2026/

**B. AEON World Mastercard 5% supermarkets: continues (1 Apr 2026 – 31 Jan 2027)** · V
- 5% at Big C (and online), Lotus's (and online), Foodland, Villa Market, Tops, Gourmet Market & Home Fresh Mart, Central Food Hall and **Makro Pro** ("Makro Pro เท่านั้น" — not Makro stores, not GO). ฿500 per principal card per cycle (primary + supplements), at ฿10,000. ## 13. New site tricks (for `research-methods.md`)

- **bangkokbank.com was rebuilt** (Adobe AEM Edge Delivery; lowercase URLs `/th/personal/cards/credit-cards/promotions/<slug>`; the old `/th-TH/…` links redirect). The old `GetCardPromotionsForSectionWithFilters` listing API is gone. **Every promotion is in one JSON**: from inside a page on the site, `fetch('/apiaem/content/bangkokbank/en/personal/cards/credit-cards/promotions.allpromo.th.json')` → `{cards:[{id,title,promotionStartDate,promotionEndDate,detailDescription,ctaLink,…}],categories,…}` (232 items on 1 Nov, 155 still live). Found with `performance.getEntriesByType('resource')`. Curl still gets a reset (Akamai); headless Chrome over CDP works. Detail pages render in ≈ 14 s; read `document.body.innerText`.
- **Visa perks API body** (captured over CDP): `POST /offers/api/portal/portal/perks/` with `{"siteId":"www.visa.co.th","perkTypeRequests":[{"requestIdentifier":null,"perkType":"OFFERS","locale":"th_TH","pageRequest":{"index":0,"limit":1000},"perkArguments":{"offerType":"U"}}]}`. An empty body gets `400 Validation failure`. Offers are under `perksGroups[0].perks[]` with `startDate`/`endDate` in ms.
- **KTC replaces a campaign at the same URL** (`ptt-station-cashback` showed Jul–Oct, now Nov–Feb). The Wayback CDX for the URL gives the previous round (`/web/<ts>id_/<url>` returns gzip — decompress before parsing). KTC pages also show "สิทธิ์ครบจำนวนแล้ว" on a quota-full box, which dates when a registration cap filled.
- **AEON promotion pages**: `aeon_tiles` from the rendered index give every slug and period in one pass (195 tiles); new campaigns appear with a start date earlier than the research date (AEON Makro started 11 Oct but wasn't in October's research) — read every tile whose period overlaps the month, not only new ones.
- **The household ledger is evidence for acceptance**: KTC UnionPay rows at 94% of a whole price (`฿629.80 = ฿670 × 0.94`) show a merchant took UnionPay QR with the 6% — `fetch-transactions` with `name_contains` per merchant, then map `card_ids` to card titles with `lib.cards.card_titles_by_id`.
- **specials.priceless.com** was in maintenance for the whole session (served `/127986/alternate/index.html`); try again later rather than concluding Mastercard has nothing.
