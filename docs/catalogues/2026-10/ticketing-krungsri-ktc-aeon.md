## 1. Offers that name Thaiticketmajor

### 1.1 Central The 1 card: "Thai Ticket Major" ฿100 cashback 🏠 (REDZ)

- **Cards**: Central The 1 card tiers `red|luxe|black|theblack`, so REDZ is included. Supplements' cashback goes to the primary account.
- **Channel**: `www.thaiticketmajor.com` and TTM's **stand-alone** outlets only ("เฉพาะสาขา Stand Alone ที่ร่วมรายการ"). Full-amount slips only.
- **Mechanic**: one slip of **฿100 or more → ฿100 cashback**; spend can't be accumulated across slips.
  - That works out to 100% on a ฿100 slip, 10% on ฿1,000 and 2% on ฿5,000.
- **Caps**: ฿100 per slip, per primary account, per month ("จำกัดเครดิตเงินคืน 100 บาท ต่อเซลล์สลิป ต่อบัญชีบัตรหลัก ต่อเดือน"). Only the **first 1,500 successful registrants each month** qualify.
- **Period**: 1 ม.ค. 69 – 31 ธ.ค. 69 (all of 2026).
- **Registration**: free in **UCHOOSE**, or by SMS. The SMS code is only on the TTM page's image, which TTM's firewall blocked.
  - Registration opens on the **1st of every month**, and you must **re-register every month**.
  - Register **on or before the transaction day**, and wait for the confirmation. Only spend after the confirmation counts.
- **Crediting**:
  - TTM's copy says within **3 days** of the transaction.
  - centralthe1card.com's copy says the credit is calculated once the merchant settles. A slip TTM hasn't settled within 30 days of the month-end doesn't count.
- **Clawback**: the cashback is recalled if TTM refunds the ticket because a show is changed or cancelled.
- **Stacking**: no "not combinable" clause in either copy.
- **URLs**:
  - https://www.centralthe1card.com/Promotion/Thai-Ticket-Major-ba3b59211acb498af487ee09e3bc5360 (code `thai-ticket-major-202601`; modal `https://www.centralthe1card.com/promotion?modalId=thai-ticket-major-202601`; modified 2026-10-03)
  - TTM's copy: https://www.thaiticketmajor.com/privilege/t1.html (headline "T1 กิน เติม เพลิน รับคืน 100 บาท")
- **Confidence**: **verified**.

### 1.2 KTC: points for 0% × 4 months (+10% cashback) at concert platforms incl. THAITICKETMAJOR 🏠

- **Cards**: every KTC credit card that earns KTC FOREVER points. Excluded: KTC CASH BACK cards, the KTC – ศูนย์บริการโลหิตแห่งชาติ card, KTC VISA CORPORATE and government cards.
  - All four household KTC cards (Digital VISA, JCB, Mastercard, UnionPay) are points cards. Whether TTM's gateway takes JCB or UnionPay isn't stated (§5).
- **Platforms** ("รายชื่อแพลตฟอร์มจองคอนเสิร์ตที่ร่วมรายการ"):
  1. All Ticket (website only)
  2. Eventpop
  3. **THAITICKETMAJOR**
  4. The Concert
  5. Ticketmaster
  6. Ticketmelon
- **Mechanic**: pay a full-amount slip of **≥ ฿3,000**, then convert it to KTC's 0% installment, paid for with points:
  - **Option 1**: points = **25% of the slip** → 0% for 4 months. Example: a ฿10,000 slip costs 2,500 points.
  - **Option 2**: points = **100% of the slip** → 0% for 4 months **plus 10% cashback**. Example: a ฿10,000 slip costs 10,000 points.
    - KTC splits the deduction: 77% of the points pay for the 10% cashback, 23% for the 0% plan. A ฿10,000 slip spends 7,700 + 2,300 points.
    - In effect: ฿0.10 a point. That's KTC's standard value (1,000 points = ฿100, [[../../promotions/ktc-forever]]), with the 0% plan thrown in.
    - Option 1 costs about ฿250 of points per ฿10,000 just for 0% × 4. It's poor value unless the installment itself is wanted.
- **Registration**: "ลงทะเบียน" box on the page, `ktc.promo/concertinstallment` (the AllTicket/Eventpop pages say `ktc.promo/allticket`), or KTC Phone 02 123 5650 — **within the transaction day**.
- **Conditions**:
  - The points must be in the account on the day of the redemption.
  - Repaying the plan early doesn't return the points.
- **Period**: 1 ม.ค. 69 – 31 ธ.ค. 69.
- **URLs**:
  - https://www.ktc.co.th/promotion/entertainment-hobby/events-concerts/concert-event-installment (lists all six platforms)
  - Per-platform copies: [eventpop-concert-and-event](https://www.ktc.co.th/promotion/entertainment-hobby/events-concerts/eventpop-concert-and-event) · [all-ticket](https://www.ktc.co.th/promotion/entertainment-hobby/events-concerts/all-ticket) · [ticketmelon-installment](https://www.ktc.co.th/promotion/entertainment-hobby/events-concerts/ticketmelon-installment)
- **Confidence**: **verified**.

**Earlier and expired KTC × TTM:** KTC's "Movie on The Beach 11" e-ticket (1 KTC FOREVER point, 400 rights) was registered through **LINE OA @Thaiticketmajor**. It ran 15–31 Aug 2026, for an event on 5 Sep 2026. **Expired.** https://www.ktc.co.th/promotion/entertainment-hobby/events-concerts/mob · verified.

What worked and what didn't, on 3 Oct.

- **thaiticketmajor.com**:

- **KTC**:
  - The live list of an event category is the category page itself: `ktc.co.th/promotion/entertainment-hobby/events-concerts` (9 slugs on 3 Oct). That is faster than sorting the sitemaps.
