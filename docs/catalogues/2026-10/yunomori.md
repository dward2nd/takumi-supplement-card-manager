## 0. Headline for a Chiang Mai household

- **There is no Yunomori branch in Chiang Mai, and none has been announced** (verified). The official site lists four branches: Sukhumvit 26, Sathorn 10 and Pattaya in Thailand, and Singapore ([contact page](https://www.yunomorionsen.com/contact-us/), WordPress API page list). The operator's announced expansion is a Bangkok flagship in Thonglor plus new KLAI / PAK outlets (sister brands, not Yunomori). So the page only matters for trips to Bangkok or Pattaya.

- Yunomori's own free **YU Privilege Club** pays **1 point per ฿25 net (1 point = ฿1 off → 4%)**. Its Silver tier, given on joining, lists **20% off spa services of 90 min or more** (§3.1). It may or may not stack with a bank deal.
- **Off-peak Exclusive** (Mon–Fri 10:00–16:00, until **31 Oct 2026**): Onsen Day Pass + ZenThai Flow 60 min, or + Foot Massage 90 min, for **฿1,190** (verified, §3.2).

### 3.1 YU Privilege Club (free membership, LINE OA) — verified

Terms: [yu-privilege-club](https://www.yunomorionsen.com/yu-privilege-club/). Benefits table: poster `wp-content/uploads/2026/08/YU-Privilege-Points_v2.jpg`. Programme page created 25 Aug 2026.

- **Join**: free, through the new LINE OA **`@yunomori.onsen`**. Requires real name, phone and birth month/year. One account per person. **50 welcome points**. A referral gives +10 points to each side, for up to 10 referrals.
- **Earn**: **1 YU point per ฿25 net, after discounts**, floored per receipt. Points post by 10:00 the next day. Give your phone number before paying. Points spent as a discount earn nothing. One member per receipt. "Points accumulation and points redemption may not be usable together with certain types of promotions".
- **Value**: 1 point = ฿1 discount coupon, so **≈4% back**. Points must first be converted into a coupon in LINE. The purchase must be at least the coupon's value. No cash, no change.
- **Point expiry**: 2 years, with a 30 June cut-off. Points from 1 Jul 2026–30 Jun 2027 expire 30 Jun 2029.

Tiers are judged on points earned in the past 12 months:

| Tier | Qualifies at | Status valid |
|---|---|---|
| **Silver** | on joining | never expires (the poster adds: unless 3 years pass with no earn or burn) |
| **Gold** | 1,000–2,199 points (≈฿25,000–54,999 of spend) | 12 months |
| **Platinum** | ≥ 2,200 points | 12 months |

## 7. Spending-plan arithmetic (for the page)

   - ฿650 walk-in, or the ฿620 voucher (4.6% less).

## Site tricks

New ways in, not yet in [[../research-methods|research methods]]:

- **yunomorionsen.com is WordPress with an open REST API.**
  - `/wp-json/wp/v2/promotions?per_page=100` lists every live promotion, with publish and modify dates and branch tags (`promotions_category`: 111 Sukhumvit, 112 Pattaya, 113 Sathorn 10, 114 Singapore).
  - `/wp-json/wp/v2/pages?per_page=100` returns the full rendered content of each branch's menu, onsen and T&C pages. The price tables are in the HTML, not images.
  - Tier tables (YU Privilege) and promo prices (฿620, ฿1,190) are in posters under `/wp-content/uploads/2026/0x/`.
- **Yunomori's online voucher shop** is ReadyPlanet `instant-bookings.com/ibs/online-voucher/?hotelkey===QTqVVN&campaignid=…`. The campaign id comes from a `/hotdeal/` page on the main site. Without a campaign id it lands on the generic IBS welcome page.
- **KTC**: grep the promotion sitemaps (`sitemap-promotions-1…12.xml`, ~12,000 URLs) for the merchant name. Then:
  - The registration short links (`ktc.co.th/wellnessmax`, `/healthlevelup`, `/beautywellness`) redirect to the full T&C page.
  - A privilege that only names an SMS keyword (e.g. `ACC`) is explained on the page its short link points to.
  - A `promotion-expired` CSS string sits in every KTC page. Check the period line or "โปรโมชั่นหมดอายุ" text instead.
- **JCB** (specialoffers.jcb): the Thailand listing renders only in a browser. A headless dump after ~15 s gives all campaign URLs (`/th/campaign/detail/<CODE>/<id>/`). Each detail page then reads with plain curl.
- **CardX**: the Meilisearch key is `MEILI_KEY` in `/static/js/main.<hash>.js`. `POST https://kong-prod-frontend.cardx.co.th/indexes/promotion/search` with `{"q":"สปา","limit":1000}` returns ~1,200 records with full template text, `effectiveDate` / `expireDate` and MCC lists. Search is fuzzy, so a Thai merchant name returns noise; filter client-side.
- **krungsricard.com**: the `/th/promotion` HTML is HTML-entity-encoded JSON (~3 MB). `html.unescape` it before grepping. The participating-merchant lists are PDFs under `/KrungsriCreditCard/media/html/Beauty_<region>.pdf`.
- **Klook** is closed to every non-app method tried (403 / captcha for curl, headless Chrome, r.jina.ai and WebFetch). Use the banks' Klook pages for codes and search snippets for prices.
- Locally, `uv run` inherits `UV_PROJECT=scripts/python`. Use `env -u UV_PROJECT uv run --no-project --with …` for throwaway tools (Pillow, OpenCV).
