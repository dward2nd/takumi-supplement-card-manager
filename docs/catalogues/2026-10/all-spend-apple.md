# Offers that name no merchant, at an Apple purchase — October 2026 (ต.ค. 69)

**Partial.** This is the report of the spend-threshold and category helper. Two other helpers stopped at the session's usage limit: one on each card's own earning at electronics MCCs, one on welcome offers. For each card's base earning use [[all-spend-ticketing]] §1, which hasn't changed. Welcome offers weren't checked.

Labels: **V** verified (official page read today) · **V(date)** taken from an earlier report · **R** reading (inference) · **U** unverified.

## 0. Headline

- **No issuer runs an October mobile/IT/electronics campaign defined only by MCC or category, except Lotus's SMP1** ("มือถือ อุปกรณ์ไอที"). Every other IT offer names merchants (see [[apple-krungsri-ktc-aeon]] and [[apple-uob]]).
- **Offers that name no merchant and count an Apple purchase**:
  - First Choice NW4;
  - Lotus's LBS3 (Power Buy only) and SMP1 / SMT2;
  - Lotus's invited Q4 segments;
  - KBank PLUSTINUM's coupon and Season 3, and LINE Points on-top;
  - AEON 365 วัน;
  - ttb so smart 1%;
  - CardX CDM3 (invitation only);
  - not held: Bangkok Bank UnionPay 2% and Titanium.
- **Almost all exclude installments.** The exceptions that count a 0% plan are KBank Season 3, LINE Points on-top and DBD, CardX CDM3, and Bangkok Bank Titanium.

## 1. First Choice NW4 (V) — [firstchoice-cashback](https://www.firstchoice.co.th/promotion/firstchoice-cashback)

- **Terms**: 1 Oct – 31 Dec 69. ฿50 at ฿5,000–9,999; ฿200 per whole ฿10,000. Caps: ฿2,000 a month and ฿6,000 for the campaign, pooled per primary account. Registered.
- **Electronics count**: the blurb names `สมาร์ทโฟน คอมพิวเตอร์`.
- **Exclusions**:
  - gold and jewellery;
  - utilities (MCC 4900 and 9402);
  - marketplaces (they go to ON4);
  - foreign-registered websites in THB. The seller of record at apple.com/th isn't stated (U); its prices include Thai VAT.
- **Installments ✗**: only `ยอดใช้จ่ายสะสมแบบเต็มจำนวน` (full-payment spend) through the credit line counts.
  - Merchant 0% plans book to the loan line, so they're out.
  - 0% Pay Later is explicitly out.
  - **U PLAN "0% สั่งได้ 3 เดือน"** (convert a full-payment slip later) isn't addressed (U). It posts a `REV-FC PLAN ON DEMAND` reversal, and NW4 drops `รายการที่ถูกยกเลิกในภายหลัง` (later-cancelled items) (R: risk).
- **Payout**: ฿45,000 alone → ฿800; ฿8,000 → ฿50. At the margin the ladder pays 2%.
- **Pointers**: First Choice **MTC4** "ผ่อนโทรศัพท์ ผ่อนโน๊ตบุ๊ค และสินค้าไอที" (`/promotion/mobile-it`, 1 Sep 69 – 28 Feb 70) names its shops. It pays on installments ≥ ฿12,000 a slip over ≥ 3 months: ฿35,000+ → ฿700 (฿800 on 24 months). Online installments and converted slips are excluded.

## 2. Lotus's (V)

- **LBS3**, electrical category: `อาทิ Power Buy, Power Mall, DAIKIN, …`. Classification is by MCC, and no MCC list is published.
  - Power Buy ✓. iStudio, Studio7/BaNANA, JIB, Advice and Apple fall under SMP1's IT category instead, so probably ✗ (R).
  - Installments ✗; Shopee, Lazada and ShopeePay ✗. Not combinable with other Lotus's promotions.
  - ฿45,000 at Power Buy → ฿500; ฿8,000 → ฿80. Pooled with Makro.
  - [page](https://www.lotussmoney.com/promotion/credit-card/shopping/cashback-nationwide-bigticket)
- **SMP1** (1 Sep – 31 Dec), "มือถือ อุปกรณ์ไอที" (mobile and IT):
  - **Covers**: Jaymart, Banana, Big Camera, Com7, I-Studio, IT City, JIB, Advice, Telewiz, plus department stores. Power Buy, marketplaces and installments are excluded. Apple and apple.com/th aren't named (U).
  - **Ladder**, highest single tier only: ฿70 per ฿3,500 (≤ ฿350), or ฿450 per ฿25,000 (≤ ฿1,800), or ฿2,600 from ฿150,000. Cap ฿2,600 a month.
  - ฿45,000 → ฿450; ฿8,000 → ฿140.
  - **Not registered.** UCHOOSE or SMS `SMP1 <16 digits>` → 081-250-7777, by the transaction day.
  - [page](https://www.lotussmoney.com/promotion/credit-card/shopping/cashback-nationwide)
- **SMT2** (1–31 Oct): ≥ ฿2,000 in the same categories → ฿200 once; first 500 accounts; register **before** spending.
  - With SMP1, ฿8,000 → ฿340 (4.25%) and ฿45,000 → ฿650.
- **Invited Q4 segments** (from the sitemap; invitations show only in UCHOOSE (U)). All exclude installments, e-wallets and marketplaces.
  - `small-ticket-a-q4-26`: ฿125 / ฿250 / ฿1,250 per slip at ≥ ฿5,000 / ≥ ฿10,000 / ≥ ฿50,000.
  - `small-ticket-b-q4-26`: ฿250 / ฿500 / ฿1,250 at ≥ ฿10,000 / ≥ ฿20,000 / ≥ ฿50,000.
  - `shopping-matching-merchant-q426-a…d`: ฿100–250 per ฿8,000–20,000, twice a month.
  - A ≥ ฿50,000 slip earns ฿1,250 (2.5%) in either small-ticket segment.

## For research-methods.md

- **Lotus's**: sort `sitemap.xml` by `<lastmod>` to find new pages. Segment tier tables are plain text in the terms.
- **UOB**: a `.json` twin keeps the terms in `promotion_tc_html`, separate from `promotion_detail_html`.
- **First Choice**: the `/promotion` index HTML lists every slug with its blurb.
- **KTC**: decode the RSC payload with `json.loads` on each `self.__next_f.push` string.
- **ttb**: the parent listing's `__NEXT_DATA__` has a `subcategories` field that lists every sub-slug.
- **Bangkok Bank**: the credit-card hub lists every live slug with its dates.
- **zsh**: quote `'--remote-allow-origins=*'` when launching Chrome.
