# Offers that name no merchant, at a hospital and a spa — October 2026

This continues [[all-spend]] (1 Oct), which ranked merchant-agnostic offers at Makro and left wallets, card networks and several banks unfinished. This report finishes those parts, and applies everything to two merchants:

- **Sriphat Medical Center** (`SRIPHAT MEDICAL CENTER CHIANGMAI TH`), the self-funded hospital of Chiang Mai University's Faculty of Medicine.
- **Yunomori Onsen & Spa**. It has **no Chiang Mai branch**; the Thai branches are Sukhumvit 26, Sathorn 10 and Pattaya (see [[yunomori]]). The spa half of this report therefore matters only on trips.

Merchant-specific offers (named-hospital cashback lists, Yunomori's own deals, the Central The 1 `HBF` category campaign) belong to the merchant reports. They are only pointed at here.

**Legend.** ✓ counts · ✗ doesn't · ? unclear · 🏠 a household card. Confidence: **verified** (official page read today) · **household** (the household's own ledger, Bureau or statements show it) · **third-party only** · **unverified**.

**MCC assumptions.**
- **Sriphat ≈ MCC 8062, not 9399/9405/7800** (household). Takumi's ฿800 on First Choice (15 Sep 2026) is linked to the Bureau row `2026M9 — NW3 cb 2%`, and September's NW3 was reconciled row by row to the UCHOOSE eligible list, to the satang (฿36,803.92, see [[../../promotions/first-choice-nw3]]). NW3 excluded government-agency spend like NW4 does, so Sriphat did not post as a government MCC there. KTC itself files Sriphat among its "public hospitals" (§4.1).
- **Yunomori ≈ MCC 7298 or 7297** (unverified). KTC files its Yunomori offer under "MCC Beauty 5698, 7230, 7297, 7298, 8011 และ 8099" ([[yunomori]]).

**Point values used.** UOB Rewards ≈ ฿0.104 (UOB → The 1) · KTC, Krungsri, KBank (at a card terminal) ฿0.10 · KBank via K PLUS ฿0.083 · The 1 ฿0.125 · LINE POINTS ฿1 · Lotus's coin ฿1 (coupon). AEON Happy Point and POINTX have no published cash value.

## 2. The household cards' own earning rules

### 2.1 UOB

- **UOB One — 1% everywhere not excluded** · verified.
  - Source: [UOB One page](https://www.uob.co.th/personal/credit-cards/cash-back/one-cash-back-credit-card.page).
  - The 1% exclusion list, verbatim: `ยอดเครดิตเงินคืน 1% ไม่นับรวมยอดใช้จ่ายที่เกิดจาก การซื้อกองทุนต่างๆ, ผลิตภัณฑ์กรมธรรม์ประกันชีวิตควบการลงทุน (“Unit link”), รายการผ่อนชำระสำหรับยอดที่ยังไม่เรียกเก็บ, ยอดเบิกเงินสดล่วงหน้า, ยอดใช้จ่ายจากสถานีบริการน้ำมัน, ยอดใช้จ่ายในห้างแม็คโคร, การชำระค่าไฟฟ้า (กฟน./กฟภ.) และค่าน้ำประปา (กปน.) …, โปรแกรมเงินสดทันใจ Fund Transfer, รายการแลกเงินตราต่างประเทศผ่านบัตรฯ, ดอกเบี้ย, ค่าปรับ, ค่าธรรมเนียมทุกประเภท, รายการที่ถูกยกเลิก…, รายการใช้จ่ายเพื่อการค้าหรือในเชิงธุรกิจ, ยอดใช้จ่ายที่เป็นสกุลเงินไทย … ร้านค้าในต่างประเทศ…, … MCC 4900 และการเติมเงินผ่านบัตร เข้า e-wallet …`
  - **No hospital, medical, government, tax or spa exclusion.** `การใช้จ่ายผ่าน e-Wallet จะได้รับเครดิตเงินคืน 1%`. The ฿2,000 cap is per statement cycle, shared by the account ([[../../promotions/uob-one-2026]]).

  - Source: [UOB World page](https://www.uob.co.th/personal/credit-cards/rewards/uob-world-credit-card.page).
  - ×5 covers `การใช้จ่ายออนไลน์, e-wallet, หมวดร้านอาหาร, หมวดท่องเที่ยว, และเงินสกุลต่างประเทศ รวมสูงสุด 20,000 บาทต่อรอบบัญชี`; everything else earns `2 เท่า สำหรับการใช้จ่ายอื่นๆ` (≈ 0.83%). The ฿20,000 quota is pooled per account ([[../../promotions/uob-world-points]]).

- **UOB Premier — ×2 domestic** (×4 only MCC 5311/5309) · verified. [UOB Premier page](https://www.uob.co.th/personal/credit-cards/shopping/uob-premier-credit-card.page). Baiboon's ฿1,620 at Sriphat (18 Oct 2025) is marked ×2 (household).

## Site tricks

New ways to read sites that [[../research-methods|research methods]] doesn't list yet:

- **UnionPay offer catalogue**: `GET https://marketing.unionpayintl.com/h5Promote/v1/merchant/getMerchantList?pageIndex=1&pageSize=50&language=en&insCode=299990156&countryCode=764` lists every Thai offer (merchant, offer number, name). `POST …/coupon/getCoupon` with JSON `{"couponno":"<offer>","couponType":"07","merchantNo":"<merchant>","language":"en","insCode":"299990156"}` returns the full terms, dates and `payMethods`. The codes (from the app bundle): `01` Mobile QuickPass (phone NFC), `02` merchant-presented QR, `03` swipe, `05` IC-card QuickPass, `06` online, `07` consumer-presented QR. `flushProcess` gives any offer's pool (already in `lib.bureau.unionpay_offer`).
- **Tencent Docs sheets** (UnionPay's merchant lists): headless `--dump-dom` hangs. Capture the page's `dop-api/opendoc` response over the DevTools protocol (the session's `cdp_capture.py`: launch Chrome with `--remote-debugging-port`, enable `Network`, save bodies whose URL matches). The cells are base64 + zlib in `clientVars.collab_client_vars.initialAttributedText.text[0].block_datas[0].related_sheet`; decompress and pull the UTF-8 strings. Repeated strings (the issuer column) are stored once, so rows can't be matched to issuers without parsing the protobuf.
- **JCB Thailand**: `https://www.specialoffers.jcb/th/campaign/output/d/southeast-and-south-asia/thailand/ajax.json` lists all Thai campaigns with category and link; detail pages read with curl.
- **Visa Thailand**: the offers page is an Angular app; its data is `https://www.visa.co.th/offers/api/portal/portal/perks/` (captured over CDP; every perk with start/end dates in milliseconds).
- **Mastercard Priceless Specials**: `POST https://specials.priceless.com/website/api/v1/search` with `{"language":"TH","sourceMarketCountry":"TH","siteInfo":"TH-TH","offset":<page 0,1,2…>,"sort":[{"properties":"categroySort","sort":"categroy"}]}` returns 9 offers a page (`offset` is a page index, not a row offset). `…/homepage` gives category counts. mastercard.co.th itself returns 403 to curl and r.jina.ai.
- **Amex Thailand** offer pages (`americanexpress.com/th-th/benefits/promotions/*.html`) read with plain curl.
- **TrueMoney promotion list**: `r.jina.ai/https://www.truemoney.com/promotion/` gives every live promotion with dates.
- **LINE BK** returns Cloudflare 403 (error 1007) to curl; `r.jina.ai` reads `/th/promotions/`.
- **Shell**: this environment sets `UV_PROJECT=scripts/python`, so a scratchpad `uv run --with websocket-client` needs `env -u UV_PROJECT uv run --no-project …`.
