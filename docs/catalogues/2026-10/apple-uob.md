# Apple products — UOB, and what wasn't read — October 2026 (ต.ค. 69)

The researcher for KBank, CardX/SCB, UOB, ttb, the other banks, the card networks and the wallets hit the session's usage limit before writing anything. UOB's pages were then read directly, because every holder has UOB One. The other issuers in that scope are listed at the end as **not read**.

UOB's iPhone hub is [iphone18.page](https://www.uob.co.th/personal/credit-cards/promotions/it-gadget/iphone18.page). Its sub-pages have no `.json` twins, so they were rendered headless. Every row is **verified**, read on 3 Oct. 🏠 UOB One (all three), UOB World, UOB Premier and UOB Makro are all "บัตรเครดิตยูโอบี ประเภทอื่น" (other UOB cards) in these tables.

## 1. Lazada, full payment — ฿5,000 off (`laz-iphone18-lpw784-0926`)

- **Mechanic**: ฿5,000 off an iPhone 18 model paid **in full**, on a slip of **≥ ฿30,000**, at Apple Authorised Resellers in the Lazada app: iStudio (copperwired), iStudio 7, iStudio by UFicon and iStudio by SPVi.
- **Period**: 12 Sep 69 (19:00) – 31 Oct 69.
- **Limits**:
  - 1 right per card account for the whole campaign.
  - **100 rights in total** for UOB Lazada cards, and **100 for every other UOB card**, counted since 12 Sep. They may already be gone.
- **Not combinable** with other promotions.
- ฿48,900 → ฿5,000 = **10.2%**.
- [page](https://www.uob.co.th/personal/credit-cards/promotions/shopping-lifestyle/laz-iphone18-lpw784-0926.page)

## 3. Power Buy — `powerbuy-iphone18-ppw723-1226` (12 Sep – 31 Oct 69)

- **Discount**: 5% off on a 0% plan of 6 or 10 months; none on 24 months.
- **Cashback per installment slip**:

  | Slip | Cashback |
  |---|---|
  | ฿10,000–24,999 | ฿150 |
  | ฿25,000–49,999 | ฿400 |
  | ฿50,000–79,999 | ฿800 |
  | ฿80,000–119,999 | ฿1,500 |
  | ฿120,000–149,999 | ฿2,400 |
  | ≥ ฿150,000 | ฿3,200 |

  The cap is ฿32,000 per person. The registration code wasn't captured.
- **Points**: redeem points worth 7× the installment amount at the terminal for **12.5%** (8,000 points → ฿1,000, so ฿0.125 a point).
- ฿48,900 on 10 months: ฿2,445 + ฿400 = **≈ 5.8%**.

## 5. Apple Store and apple.com/th — `apple-store-iphone-18-ipw720-1226` (12 Sep – 31 Dec 69)

- **Cashback**: 1% on a 0% plan of up to 10 months, slip ≥ ฿30,000. Cap ฿10,000 per person.
- **Points**: redeem UOB Rewards for **12%** cashback (SMS `18BPO <12 digits> <points>` → 4545111). Limit 100,000 points per card number.
- Counts only the app and website channels.
