**Confidence labels**:
- **verified**: the official page was read on 2026-10-02.
- **verified (archived)**: the official page was read as a Wayback Machine copy, with the capture date given.
- **third-party only**: the only source is not the issuer or the merchant.

**Sriphat's own website was unreachable from this machine.** `sriphat.med.cmu.ac.th` reset every connection: TLS `SSL_ERROR_SYSCALL`, an empty reply on port 80, nothing from headless Chrome or WebFetch, and HTTP 422 from `r.jina.ai`. ## 1. What the two places are

### 1.1 Sriphat Medical Center (ศูนย์ศรีพัฒน์ คณะแพทยศาสตร์ มหาวิทยาลัยเชียงใหม่)

- **History** (Sriphat's `ประวัติความเป็นมา` page, verified (archived) 21 Apr 2026, https://sriphat.med.cmu.ac.th/th/history):
  - The CMU council approved it on 29 Jun 1996 as `ศูนย์บริการสุขภาพพิเศษ มหาวิทยาลัยเชียงใหม่` (a special health-service centre), set up as an `องค์กรในกํากับของมหาวิทยาลัย` (an organisation under the university's supervision) and overseen by the Faculty of Medicine.
  - It was renamed `สถานบริการสุขภาพพิเศษ มหาวิทยาลัยเชียงใหม่` on 18 Jul 1997.
  - After CMU left the civil-service system, it became `ศูนย์ศรีพัฒน์ คณะแพทยศาสตร์ มหาวิทยาลัยเชียงใหม่`, "a unit of the Faculty of Medicine", on 30 Dec 2008.
- **So it is the Faculty of Medicine's fee-paying "special" (private-wing) hospital**, inside a public autonomous university. It sits at 110/392 ถ.อินทวโรรส, on the Suan Dok campus next to Maharaj Nakorn Chiang Mai Hospital.
  - Its 2025 logo reads `โรงพยาบาลศูนย์ศรีพัฒน์ / SRIPHAT MEDICAL CENTER HOSPITAL`.
  - The site header used a monochrome `SRIPHAT HOSPITAL CMU` mark in 2026 (§7).
- **How the banks classify it**: KTC lists it as a **government hospital**. It is no. 9 on KTC's `รายชื่อโรงพยาบาลรัฐบาลที่ร่วมรายการ` (participating government hospitals) for MKTHO-1399 (§4.1): `9. ศูนย์ศรีพัฒน์ คณะแพทยศาสตร์ มหาวิทยาลัยเชียงใหม่`. **verified**
- **Its MCC is published nowhere.** No bank page, forum post or MCC directory gives it.
  - **The household's evidence points to MCC 8062 (hospitals), not 9399 (government services)**:
    - First Choice NW3 excludes government bodies and "some public hospitals" (MCC 9399, 9405, 7800).
    - Takumi's `SRIPHAT MEDICAL CENTER CHIANGMAI TH` ฿800 (15 Sep 2026, First Choice) is linked to the Bureau's `2026M9 — NW3` row.
    - That row matched the UCHOOSE app's NW3 eligible list to the satang (see [[../../promotions/first-choice-nw3]]). So the bank counted it, which a 9399 posting would not get.
    - Baiboon's UOB Premier earned the ×2 base on it (฿1,620, 18 Oct 2025).
  - **unverified** (household evidence, not a statement MCC).

### 1.2 Center for Medical Excellence (ศูนย์ความเป็นเลิศทางการแพทย์, "CMEx")

- **A Faculty of Medicine centre, separate from Sriphat.** Its 2023 home page (verified (archived), https://excellent.med.cmu.ac.th/meccmu/, capture 22 Mar 2023) lists:
  - the Lasik centre, PET/CT, the Women Health centre, `ศูนย์ดูแลผู้สูงอายุระยะยาว` (LTCC, long-term elderly care) and health-check programmes;
  - forms for `ใบรับรองการตรวจวินิจฉัยสำหรับเบิกกรมบัญชีกลาง` (Comptroller-General civil-servant reimbursement), which suggests a public facility.
- **Third-party listings** (third-party only):
  - The outpatient clinic is on the 3rd floor of `อาคารเฉลิมพระเกียรติ 7 รอบ`, tel. 053-934710.
  - Its Facebook page is `CMExClinic` (https://www.facebook.com/CMExClinic/).
  - Wikipedia's Maharaj Nakorn Chiang Mai article names the Lasik, CLCS, PET/CT, Women's Health and Sleep centres as Maharaj's "Centres for Excellence".
- **So CMEx is part of the public university hospital (Maharaj / the Faculty of Medicine), not of Sriphat.** It is **not** on KTC's MKTHO-1399 government-hospital list. **unverified** (inference)
- **MCC — household evidence**:
  - `CENTER FOR MEDICAL EXC… CHIANGMAI TH` on Baiboon's KTC UnionPay earned no points (Aug 2026, …2310). KTC rule 16 gives UnionPay nothing at MCC 8062 *and* 9399 (see [[../../promotions/ktc-forever]]), so this alone can't tell the two apart.
  - Baiboon's ฿450 on First Choice (25 Sep 2026) is linked to the reconciled `2026M9 — NW3` row, which points away from 9399.
  - Best reading: MCC 8062. **unverified**
- **CMEx takes UnionPay QR.** Baiboon's KTC UnionPay row of 6 Aug 2026 is ฿423, which is ฿450 less the 6% QR discount. **unverified** (household evidence)

## 4. Bank, network and wallet promotions

**Two kinds**:
- (A) programmes that **name Sriphat or Maharaj**;
- (B) programmes that pay at **any hospital by MCC**, which reach Sriphat and CMEx only if the household's MCC 8062 reading is right.

Every hospital list in (C) was checked, and none names Sriphat or CMEx.

### 4.1 (A) Named: KTC government-hospital installments, MKTHO-1399 · **verified**

- **Pays**: `โปรโมชั่นผ่อนชำระค่ารักษาพยาบาล ดอกเบี้ยพิเศษ 0.69% นานสูงสุด 10 เดือน ที่โรงพยาบาลรัฐบาลที่ร่วมรายการ`.
  - **It is not 0%**: 0.69% a month for 10 months, and KTC credits back the first 3 months' interest.
  - So it costs about 4.8% of the bill (7 × 0.69%) if the rate is on the principal, as is usual for KTC.
  - The page footer says "0% นานสูงสุด 10 เดือน", but the terms say 0.69%.
- **Period**: 1 Jan – 31 Dec 2026. `รับสิทธิ์ได้ไม่ต้องลงทะเบียน` (no registration).
- **Who**: any KTC credit card except KTC VISA CORPORATE and government-agency cards.
- **Rule**: a full-amount slip of ≥ ฿3,000 at the listed hospitals. Call KTC PHONE 02 123 5000, press 4, **before the statement date** of the cycle.
- **Crediting**: the interest refund lands within 60 days of the posting date.
- **No KTC points** on the converted slip, for all 10 months.
- **Extras**: KTC donates 0.20% of the installment to the hospital. KTC FOREVER points can also be donated (1,000 points → ฿100, and KTC adds ฿100).
- **The list (12)**: Siriraj, Ramathibodi, Kanchanaphisek (Siriraj), Phramongkutklao, Mahidol Dental, Chulalongkorn, Songklanagarind, `คลินิกศูนย์แพทย์พัฒนา`, **`ศูนย์ศรีพัฒน์ คณะแพทยศาสตร์ มหาวิทยาลัยเชียงใหม่`**, Chulabhorn, Suan Benjakitti, Thammasat.
  - Maharaj Nakorn Chiang Mai and CMEx are **not** on it.
- **URL**: https://www.ktc.co.th/promotion/health-beauty/hospital/installment-plan-top-public-hospitals

  - UCHOOSE shows which slips can convert, which is the practical test.
- **URLs**: https://www.centralthe1card.com/promotion?modalId=u-plan-202601 · https://www.firstchoice.co.th/promotion/epr-hospital

## Site tricks

New ones, not yet in [[../research-methods]]:
- **`sriphat.med.cmu.ac.th` and `excellent.med.cmu.ac.th` drop every connection from the HK egress**: curl, headless Chrome, WebFetch and `r.jina.ai` all fail. Use the **Wayback CDX API** instead:
  - list the pages with `web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=sriphat.med.cmu.ac.th/th*&output=json&filter=statuscode:200&collapse=urlkey`;
  - fetch the raw HTML with `/web/<timestamp>id_/<url>` and images with `/web/<timestamp>im_/<url>`;
  - `/web/2026id_/<url>` jumps to the capture nearest 2026.
  - Sriphat's package **covers** (`/images/package/<id>/cover_<id>.jpg`) are archived and carry the prices; the detail posters (`TH<epoch>.png`) are not.
- **UOB's `.json` twins**: the path is `/assets/web-resources/personal/credit-cards/promotions/<category>/<page>.json`, the last two segments of the `.page` URL (from `promotions-details-v2.js`). `/personal/promotions/credit-cards/<slug>/<slug>.page` pages have no twin at the same pattern (404).
- **UnionPay offer pages render in headless Chrome** (`--virtual-time-budget=25000`). The hospital offer links its merchant list as a **Tencent Docs sheet**:
  - `curl` the sheet URL with a cookie jar; its HTML holds only the first rows (`flyweight.texts`).
  - Then call `https://docs.qq.com/dop-api/opendoc?id=<docId>&tab=<tab>&outformat=1&normal=1&startrow=0&endrow=400&wb=1&nowb=0` with the same cookies and the sheet as the Referer.
  - `clientVars.collab_client_vars.initialAttributedText.text[0].related_sheet` is **base64-encoded zlib**. Decompress it and pull the UTF-8 strings to get the whole list.
- **The CardX search key**: `MEILI_KEY` sits in the main JS bundle's config object (`MEILI:"https://kong-prod-frontend.cardx.co.th", MEILI_KEY:"25bb7965…074471"`). Use it as `Authorization: Bearer <key>` on `POST /indexes/promotion/search` with `{"q": "...", "limit": N}`. Meilisearch is typo-tolerant, so a Thai query like `ศรีพัฒน์` can return an unrelated hit; check the text.
- **KTC hospital merchant lists** are PDFs linked from each page (`/upload/02-promotion-download…/…pdf`), and `pdftotext -layout` reads them. Every KTC hospital page's terms also state the hospital MCC rules for earning.
- **Krungsri hospital lists** are PDFs under `/KrungsriCreditCard/media/html/` (`HOS_2026.pdf`, `hospital-deal-<region>.pdf`). First Choice's are `/getattachment/<guid>/Dealer-Hos_KFC.pdf`.
- **Logos and payment info without the merchant's site**:
  - `graph.facebook.com/<page>/picture?type=large&width=720` returns the Facebook profile picture to plain curl (302 to fbcdn).
  - `page.line.me/<LINE OA id>` (found in the site's `line.me/R/ti/p/%40…` link) is readable with curl. Its "Basic info" lists accepted payments (cash, card brands, contactless).
- **Bangkok Bank**: the `th-TH` promotion URLs render in headless Chrome.
