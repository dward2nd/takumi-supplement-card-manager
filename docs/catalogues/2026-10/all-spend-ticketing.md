# Offers that name no merchant, at a ticket agency — October 2026 (ต.ค. 69)

This continues [[all-spend]] (1 Oct, Makro) and [[all-spend-hospital-spa]] (2 Oct, hospital and spa). It applies the same offers to **Thaiticketmajor** (TTM, `บริษัท ไทยทิคเก็ตเมเจอร์ จำกัด`), Thailand's biggest ticket agency. On statements it posts as `WWW.THAITICKETMAJOR.CO BANGKOK THA` (web) or `THAITICKETMAJOR(TTMEVE BANGKOK THA`.

Offers that name TTM (KBank ฿200, CardX ฿200, Central The 1 ฿100, KTC's points-for-0%) belong to the merchant report. They're listed in §8 only so the plan can compare.

**Legend.** ✓ counts · ✗ doesn't · ? unclear · 🏠 a household card. A **reading** is my inference from the terms, labelled as such.

**Point values used** (the same as [[all-spend-hospital-spa]]): UOB Rewards ≈ ฿0.104 (UOB → The 1) · KTC, Krungsri, KBank at a card terminal ฿0.10 · KBank via K PLUS ฿0.083 · The 1 ฿0.125 · LINE POINTS ฿1 · Lotus's coin ฿1 (coupon). AEON Happy Point and POINTX have no published cash value.

## 0. Thaiticketmajor's payment terms (they change every rate)

- **Online methods**, as one event page lists them: `บัตรเครดิต, บัตรเดบิต, K Pay Plus, True Money Wallet, Shopee Pay, Alipay, Wechat pay , QR Promtpay (Processing fee 3%)` ([BIGBANG page](https://www.thaiticketmajor.com/concert/bigbang-2026-2027-world-tour-xx-cosmos-in-bangkok.html)) · verified.
  - **No LINE Pay and no UnionPay QR** among them. TTM's QR is a PromptPay QR.
  - The card brands accepted (Visa / Mastercard / JCB / UnionPay) aren't listed anywhere: "บัตรเครดิต" only · not found.
- **A 3% processing fee on cards, wallets and PromptPay QR.**
  - 9 of the 14 event pages read today state it, for example: `การชำระเงินผ่านบัตรเครดิต, บัตรเดบิต, True Money Wallet, ShopeePay, Alipay, WeChat Pay , QR PromptPay จะมี processing fee 3%` ([JAN JINGJING fancon](https://www.thaiticketmajor.com/concert/jan-jingjing-bewitch-you-fancon.html), [LANY](https://www.thaiticketmajor.com/concert/lany-soft-world-tour-bangkok.html), [Joji](https://www.thaiticketmajor.com/concert/joji-solaris-tour-2026.html)). The other 5 didn't mention payment at all.
  - One event charges **0.5% for `K Pay Plus, BBL iBanking`** and takes **cash at TTM counters** with no fee; cards at the counter still pay 3% ([FAYE PERAYA party](https://www.thaiticketmajor.com/concert/faye-peraya-birthday-party-2026-1410-static.html)).
  - TTM's refund policy excludes `Payment services fee 3% ของบัตรเครดิต/เดบิต,TRUE WALLET, AIRPAY (ถ้ามี)` from refunds, on top of the `ค่าธรรมเนียมในการออกบัตร 30 บาท / บัตรการแสดง 1 ใบ` that every channel pays ([policies](https://corporate.thaiticketmajor.com/policies.php)) · verified.
  - Several events also say `รองรับการผ่อนชำระผ่านบัตรเครดิต/เดบิต`. Installments would cancel most of the cashback below.
- **Older channels** on the [how-to-pay page](https://corporate.thaiticketmajor.com/how-to-pay.php): cash at banks and counters (fee "ขึ้นอยู่กับผู้ให้บริการ"), mPAY (฿15 a transaction), Bangkok Bank ATM and Bualuang iBanking direct debit, ShopeePay by scanning a QR on screen, TrueMoney by phone number + OTP, Cross-Border ThaiQR · verified (undated page).
- **Chiang Mai outlets**: Major Central Airport Chiang Mai (4th floor), Major Central Festival Chiang Mai (5th floor), Lotus's Hang Dong / Kham Thiang / Ruam Chok / Fang / Chom Thong, and the Chiang Mai and CMU post offices ([outlet list](https://corporate.thaiticketmajor.com/outlet-location.php)) · verified. Big concerts often sell online only on the first day (BIGBANG: online only on 6 Sep, all channels from 7 Sep).

**Reading — card vs the cheapest way to pay.** Paying by card adds 3% to the price. So a card's reward has to beat that 3% before it saves anything against cash at a counter (where an event allows cash).
- Online with the fee on every method, the cards just compete with each other.
- Where an event offers K Pay Plus at 0.5%, a card breaks even only above about **2.4% of the charge** (1 − 1.005/1.03).
- Every rate below is on the card **charge**, which already includes the 3% fee. On a ฿3,000 ticket that charge is **฿3,090**. Whether the fee and the ticket post as one slip isn't stated; the examples assume one.

## 2. Verdicts on the brief's questions

### 2.1 Krungsri NOW 5% online — ✓ counts (reading) · verified terms
- Terms ([NOW page](https://www.krungsricard.com/th/product/creditcard/now), all 2026): `รับเครดิตเงินคืน 5% เมื่อซื้อสินค้า/บริการผ่านเว็บไซต์หรือแอปพลิเคชัน`, `เมื่อใช้จ่ายครบทุกๆ 500 บาท/เซลล์สลิป จำกัดเครดิตเงินคืนสูงสุด 300 บาท/เดือน`. The headline names `ช้อป กิน ดูหนัง ฟังเพลง เติมเกม`.
- The travel exclusion is narrow: `ยกเว้นหมวดประกัน หมวดกองทุน และหมวดท่องเที่ยว รวมถึง โรงแรมและที่พัก ตั๋วการเดินทาง/เครื่องบิน/รถไฟ เช่ารถ และบริษัทท่องเที่ยว`. The tickets it excludes are **travel tickets (air, rail)**, not event tickets.
- Also out: `ไม่รวมการซื้อโฆษณาออนไลน์และการใช้จ่ายผ่าน QR Code`, utilities, transit, petrol, government MCCs, charities, easyBills, Krungsri Smart Plan installments.
- **Reading:** a card-not-present card payment on www.thaiticketmajor.com is a website purchase and counts. Paying TTM's PromptPay QR or ShopeePay QR does not.
- Credited within 5 days of posting, as the bank's `CB <merchant>` line; the row earns no points (`สงวนสิทธิ์ยกเว้นการให้คะแนนสะสม กรุงศรี พอยต์ สำหรับทุกรายการใช้จ่าย`). `ไม่สามารถใช้ร่วมกับรายการส่งเสริมการขายอื่นๆ ได้` is on the page, but the household saw NOW stack with SUP1 in September ([[all-spend]]).
- Optimum: slips in whole ฿500 steps up to ฿6,000 of charge a month. The ฿300 is shared with every other NOW online purchase that month; how much is left shows on the Bureau's October NOW row.

### 2.2 First Choice NW4 — ✓ counts · verified + household
- Terms ([NW4 page](https://www.firstchoice.co.th/promotion/firstchoice-cashback), 1 Oct – 31 Dec): ฿5,000–9,999 → ฿50; every whole ฿10,000 → ฿200; ≤ ฿2,000 a month, ≤ ฿6,000 for the campaign, per primary account; registered.
- **Its exclusions name no entertainment, event or ticket category.** The travel exclusion is `หมวดท่องเที่ยว หมายรวมถึงการซื้อตั๋วเครื่องบิน การจองโรงแรม และยอดจองผ่าน Travel Agency ต่างๆ บริการจองรถ เช่ารถ และหมวดสินค้าปลอดภาษี (Duty Free)` (→ TR3).
  - That covers air tickets and travel agencies. It would matter only if TTM posted as 4722, and nothing suggests it does.
  - Also out: marketplaces (→ ON4), delivery (→ DLV3), insurance, MCC 5199, government 9399/9405/7800, foreign or foreign-registered merchants, e-wallet top-ups, installments, auto-debits.
- TTM is a Thai company billing in baht (`BANGKOK THA`), so the foreign-site rule doesn't bite.
- **Reading:** `ดอกเบี้ย ค่าปรับ ค่าธรรมเนียมต่างๆ` means card fees. TTM's 3% processing fee is part of the merchant's charge and counts with the ticket.
- Value: 2% at the margin. A ticket that pushes the month's pooled spend past the next whole ฿10,000 earns ฿200; one that doesn't earns nothing yet. Tracked by `nw4.py`.

### 2.3 Krungsri ONQ3 — ✗ by card · ✓ through TrueMoney or ShopeePay (reading) · verified terms
- Terms ([ONQ3 page](https://www.krungsricard.com/th/promotion/online-shopping-cashback), 7 Aug – 30 Nov): only `ร้านค้าช้อปออนไลน์ ได้แก่ Lazada, Shopee, TikTok Shop, LINE SHOPPING` and `แอปอีวอลเล็ต ได้แก่ TrueMoney, LINE Pay, ShopeePay`, excluding delivery apps paid by wallet.
- Monthly ladder ฿40 / ฿170 / ฿350 at ฿3,000 / ฿15,000 / ฿30,000, per primary card account (VISA, JCB and Lady separate); **Krungsri NOW is excluded**; registered on VISA/JCB/Lady.
- A direct card payment at TTM isn't a listed shop → ✗. TTM accepts TrueMoney and ShopeePay, so a Krungsri card paid through either counts toward the ladder (reading).
- The same page has **"คุ้ม 2"**: points-to-cashback `PT26`, 500 points → ฿50 under a list of MCCs. That is Krungsri's ordinary ฿0.10 a point, and the list (`MCC-Code-10percent-Y2026_04-08-26.pdf`) has **no 7922** (§4).

### 2.4 UOB SPW796 — ✗ by card · ✓ through ShopeePay or TrueMoney (reading) · verified terms
- Terms (`shopping-online-spw796-1226.json`, 1 Oct – 31 Dec): `เฉพาะยอดใช้จ่ายเต็มจำนวนผ่านแอปพลิเคชัน Shopee, Lazada, TikTok, LINE Shopping, Central App, King Power, Shopee Pay, True money และ LINE PAY`.
- It is **a named-app list, not an MCC list**: a ticket site paid by card isn't "e-commerce" here.
- ฿100 per whole ฿5,000 pooled a month, ≤ ฿200 a month and ฿600 for the campaign per cardholder; registered (`EC`).
- Out: `การชำระบิล ค่าประกัน ค่าภาษี ค่าบริการสาธารณูปโภคทุกประเภท Easy Pass`, fuel through any wallet, wallet top-ups, LINE MAN and ShopeeFood.
- **Reading:** TTM's TrueMoney and ShopeePay checkouts are merchant payments, not bill payments, so they count. Paying a TTM order through mPAY's `ชำระบิล` menu would not.

### 2.5 UOB World ×5 — ✓ ×5 (reading + household) · verified terms
- Terms ([UOB World page](https://www.uob.co.th/personal/credit-cards/rewards/uob-world-credit-card.page), rendered headless today): 5 points per ฿25 for `การใช้จ่ายออนไลน์, e-wallet, หมวดร้านอาหาร, หมวดท่องเที่ยว, และเงินสกุลต่างประเทศ รวมสูงสุด 20,000 บาทต่อรอบบัญชี`, otherwise 2.
- The online bonus is defined by the network flag, not by MCC: `ธนาคารขอสงวนสิทธิ์การให้คะแนนสะสม 5 เท่า เฉพาะรายการที่ถูกกำหนดให้เป็นรายการ e-Commerce โดยมาสเตอร์การ์ดและวีซ่าเท่านั้น`. UOB i-Plan installments earn ×2.
- **Household:** Takumi's two TTM rows on UOB World, `WWW.THAITICKETMAJOR.CO BANGKOK THA` ฿6,270 (12 Feb 2025) and `THAITICKETMAJOR(TTMEVE BANGKOK THA` ฿100 (31 Jan 2025), are both marked `×5`. That is the household's marking; the points columns are formulas over it, and the Feb 2025 statement line wasn't checked.
- The ฿20,000 quota counts every transaction on the account, Takumi's and Baiboon's (`uob_world.py`). Through TrueMoney/ShopeePay it is ×5 too (e-wallet category).

### 2.6 UOB One 1%, ttb so smart 1%, KBank LINE Points 1% — ✓ · verified

### 2.7 KTC — 0.4% on every card; KTC UnionPay hinges on 7922 vs 7999 · verified
- From the verbatim terms ([[../../sources/ktc-forever-terms-2026-09-28.txt|sources/ktc-forever-terms-2026-09-28.txt]]): 1 point per ฿25, 1,000 points = ฿100.

  - Rule (14) (Japan travel, incl. 7996) and rule (15) (Visa B2B, incl. 7829 film production) don't reach a Thai ticket agency.
- KTC's 2569 notice (effective 1 May 2026) adds the B2B, Japan-travel, New Zealand and Mastercard-TrueMoney rules, still with no 7922 ([notice PDF](https://www.ktc.co.th/upload/announcement-document/KTC2569-Notice-Changes-KTC-Point-Cashback-Earning-en.pdf)) · verified.

### 2.8 CardX POINTX online (`OSD`) — ✗ by card · ✓ through a wallet (reading) · verified terms
- Terms (CardX index, `thematic-day-jun26-onc05`, 1 Jun – 31 Dec): redeem POINTX equal to the slip → **14%**, or **20%** on Special Days (`Double Day 10.10 : 9 ต.ค. 69 – 11 ต.ค. 69`, `Payday : 25 – ทุกสิ้นเดือน`).
  - ≤ 5,000 points a person a day; register each time, the same day: SMS `OSD <12 digits> <slip amount>` → 4545777. CardX FAMILY PLUS is excluded.
- **Participating merchants are a named list in 5 categories**:
  - E-Marketplace (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, LINE Shopping, RS Mall, Topvalue);
  - Delivery & Transportation;
  - `E-Wallet เฉพาะรายการชำระค่าสินค้า … Line Pay , True Money Wallet , Shopee Pay`;
  - Streaming & Life Style (Monomax, VIU, TrueID, Horoworld, Doxzilla);
  - Online Travel Agents under MCC 4722/7011.
- **Thaiticketmajor isn't on it** → ✗ by card. Paid through TrueMoney or ShopeePay it falls in the E-Wallet category (reading).
