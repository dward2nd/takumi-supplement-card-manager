Researched 3 Oct 2026, 01:00–03:30 ICT. No Notion reads or writes.

Confidence: **verified** = the issuer's (or, where said, TTM's) official page or data was read · **third-party only** · **unverified**. 🏠 = a household card.

Household cards in scope: 🏠 KBank JCB, KBank LINE Points (Visa), KBank PLUSTINUM (Visa), KBank Shopee (Visa) — all Takumi's; 🏠 CardX JCB (Nuta); 🏠 UOB One (all three), UOB World and UOB Premier (Takumi + Baiboon), UOB Makro (Takumi); 🏠 ttb so smart (Takumi + Baiboon). ## 5. SPW796 and UOB World ×5 on a TTM web purchase

- **UOB World ×5**: the card page says `สำหรับการรับคะแนนสะสมพิเศษในหมวดออนไลน์ ธนาคารขอสงวนสิทธิ์การให้คะแนนสะสม 5 เท่า เฉพาะรายการที่ถูกกำหนดให้เป็นรายการ e-Commerce โดยมาสเตอร์การ์ดและวีซ่าเท่านั้น` ([page](https://www.uob.co.th/personal/credit-cards/rewards/uob-world-credit-card.page)). A card-not-present order on thaiticketmajor.com or ticketmaster.co.th is normally flagged e-commerce, so **×5 is likely online** (counting against the ฿20,000 cycle quota) and **×2 at a TTM outlet**. No household TTM row on UOB World has been checked against a statement · verified (rule) / unverified (flag).
- **SPW796** (1 Oct – 31 Dec, SMS `EC`): counts only full-amount spend through Shopee, Lazada, TikTok, LINE Pay, LINE Shopping, Central App, King Power, ShopeePay and TrueMoney ([[../../promotions/uob-epw538]], `scripts/python/lib/bureau/spw796.py`). **A TTM order paid by card does not count.** It would count only if paid through True Wallet or ShopeePay (both accepted by TTM) funded by a UOB card and booked by UOB as a TrueMoney/ShopeePay transaction — whether that works at TTM is unverified. Paying that way keeps UOB One's 1% and UOB World's e-wallet ×5 but loses ttb so smart's 1% and probably the KBank and CardX TTM deals (they require paying with their card at TTM).

## 6. Stacking

- KBank §1.1 and CardX §1.2 are different issuers, so one person can take both in a month on different slips (KBank ≥ ฿200, CardX ≥ ฿1,000).
- CardX §1.2 + §1.3 on the same slip: not stated. KBank §1.1 + Maroon 5 draw rights + PLUSTINUM thresholds: nothing in the terms forbids it; the draw and the coupons count all spend.
- SCB debit (§1.4) and Bangkok Bank (§1.5) say they can't be combined with other promotions.
