### 1.1 UNQ — cashback per slip (พิเศษ 1) · V

Cards: every Krungsri Card credit card, Visa / Mastercard / JCB (🏠 Krungsri VISA, Krungsri JCB, Krungsri Lady, Krungsri NOW, all registered). Excluded: Krungsri Corporate Card, TCC Privilege Card, Krungsri HomePro Corporate Card. The terms speak only of "บัตรเครดิต กรุงศรี", so **First Choice, Central The 1 and Lotus's cards are not in UNQ** (R: none of them is named, and UNIQLO lists no page for them).

Channel: every UNIQLO store, www.uniqlo.com and the UNIQLO app. No department-store-branch exclusion (unlike UOB's UNO).

| Slip (full amount, one sales slip) | Krungsri JCB, every type | Other Krungsri cards |
|---|---|---|
| ฿3,000 – 5,999 | ฿150 | ฿150 |
| ฿6,000 – 8,999 | ฿300 | ฿300 |
| ฿9,000 – 9,999 | ฿450 | ฿450 |
| ฿10,000 or more | **฿800** ("8%, equal to ฿800") | **฿700** ("7%, equal to ฿700") |
| Cap per **primary card account** per month | ฿800 | ฿700 |
| Cap per primary card account, whole campaign | ฿4,000 | ฿3,500 |

- The text names the ฿800 cards as Krungsri JCB Platinum, Siam Takashimaya JCB and Krungsri JCB Ultimate; the banner says "บัตรเครดิต กรุงศรี เจซีบี ทุกประเภท" (every Krungsri JCB). 🏠 Takumi's Krungsri JCB is a JCB Platinum, so ฿800.
- Per slip: "ยอดใช้จ่ายแต่ละรายการไม่สามารถนำมาสะสมหรือรวมกันได้" — slips never add up.
- Supplements count with their primary ("คำนวณยอดใช้จ่ายจากทั้งบัตรหลักและบัตรเสริมรวมกัน"); the credit goes to the primary account only.
- **Registration: once** ("ลงทะเบียนเพียงครั้งเดียวรับสิทธิ์ตลอดรายการ"), within the day of the purchase, in UCHOOSE (search `UNQ`) or SMS `UNQ <16-digit card no.>` → **081-927-9999**; wait for the confirmation SMS.
- Crediting: within 30 days after each month-end, to the primary card account. A slip the merchant doesn't bill within 30 days of the month-end doesn't count; cancelled slips don't count.
- Exclusions: UNQ's own box lists only cancelled and unbilled slips; the page's general terms (printed in the points box) add foreign currency, interest/fees, cancelled or refunded slips, charges not on the statement, **Krungsri Consumer Installment Plan**, business use, own-EDC charges (R: read as applying to UNQ too, as the repo does).
- **Not combinable**: "ไม่สามารถใช้ร่วมกับรายการส่งเสริมการขายอื่นๆ ได้" is printed in the points boxes (พิเศษ 2 and 3), not in UNQ's own box; the repo reads it as covering UNQ (R).
- Matches the repo (`lib/bureau/uniqlo.py`, `docs/promotions/uniqlo-2026.md`) on tiers, caps, registration and crediting. **New detail**: the campaign caps are ฿4,000 (JCB) and ฿3,500 (others) per primary account, i.e. 5× the month, as the repo already assumed.

### 1.2 Points → cashback "10%" (พิเศษ 2) · V

- Every Krungsri card (primary only) with enough points: 500 points → ฿50, 1,000 → ฿100, 5,000 → ฿500, redeemed in **UCHOOSE** within the month of the spend. No minimum spend. ≤ 500,000 points (฿50,000) per primary account per day. Credited the next day. Runs 1 Jan – 31 Dec 2026 (so it ends before UNQ does).
- This is Krungsri's ordinary **฿0.10 a point** (`PT26`, see [[krungsri-group]] and [[health-beauty-spa]]), shown on UNIQLO's page; it isn't a UNIQLO bonus. UNIQLO's page says the spend must fall "under the MCC codes defined"; the PT26 MCC list is checked in §1.5.
- Same page: "not combinable with other promotions" boilerplate.

### 1.3 Krungsri JCB "J SHOPPING" 3× / JCB Ultimate 5× points (พิเศษ 3) · V

| Card | Total points | Bonus cap | Period |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🏠 Krungsri JCB Platinum | **3×** (1× normal + 2× bonus), no minimum per slip, no registration | 400 bonus points / primary account / month, every participating brand together | 1 Oct 2026 – 31 Dec 2027; **UNIQLO only 1 Oct 2026 – 28 Feb 2027** |
| Krungsri JCB Ultimate | **5×** (1× + 4×), no minimum | 1,600 bonus points / primary account / month | **21 Oct 2026** – 31 Dec 2027 (UNIQLO's page) |

- Brands: UNIQLO, ISSEY MIYAKE (and IM MEN, PLEATS PLEASE, HOMME PLISSÉ, BAO BAO, me), COMME des GARÇONS / CDGCDGCDG (Bangkok branches only), LOFT, MATSUKIYO, NITORI, TSURUHA, UFM FUJI SUPER.
- Bonus points arrive within 5 days of the charge posting; a supplement's go to the primary account.
- "Not combinable with other promotions" appears in this box too. Read with UNQ's own line, the household's reading should be that the bank may treat the 3× and UNQ as exclusive; neither page says which wins (U).

It looks like a card launching on 21 Oct (U).

### 1.4 Points → 13% / 15% cashback in Central malls' plaza zone ("all-plaza") · V for the terms, R for UNIQLO

Krungsri's **"แลกรับเครดิตเงินคืนสูงสุด 15%* ที่ศูนย์การค้าและแบรนด์แฟชั่นชั้นนำ"**, **1 Oct – 31 Dec 2026**, covers **every shop in the plaza zone ("ทุกร้านค้าในโซนพลาซ่า")** of the listed malls, which include **Central Festival Chiangmai** and **Central Plaza Chiangmai Airport** (and every other Central mall, Terminal21, Fashion Island, The Promenade, One Bangkok Retail …). <https://www.krungsricard.com/th/promotion/all-plaza>

- UNIQLO isn't named, but it's a plaza-zone tenant in those malls and isn't in the exclusions (department stores themselves, Central App / central.co.th / ONESIAM, King Power, supermarkets and food halls, food courts, furniture/electrical chains, car services, parking, hotels, couriers, airlines, e-wallets, gift cards, utilities). So a UNIQLO slip at Central Chiangmai or Chiang Mai Airport reads as eligible (**R**; the bank decides by its own merchant mapping). uniqlo.com doesn't count (only listed fashion brands count online, and UNIQLO isn't one).
- Mechanic: slip ≥ **฿1,000**; redeem Krungsri points **up to the slip amount**, after paying and within the day: **13% Mon–Thu, 15% Fri–Sun** (฿0.13 / ฿0.15 a point vs ฿0.10 normally). Slips don't add up.
- Redeem by USSD **`*465*12581*<last 4 digits>*<points>#`** from the phone registered with the bank. Primary account only; a supplement's slip is redeemed from the primary.
- Cap **100,000 points per person a month** across all participating shops. Credit within 90 days after the month.
- Cards: every Krungsri card except Corporate / TCC Privilege / HomePro Corporate. The listing's `CardType` names Krungsri Platinum, NOW Platinum, Lady Titanium and JCB Platinum: all four 🏠 Krungsri cards.
- The page has no "not combinable" line (unlike UNQ's). Whether the bank pays UNQ cashback and this redemption on the same slip is **U**.
- Its Bangkok twin `premium-plaza-fashion` (13% / 15%, 18% from ฿50,000 on Private Banking / Exclusive Signature / Signature; USSD `*465*12647*…#`) covers Emporium/EmQuartier/EmSphere, Paragon, Siam Discovery/Center, ICONSIAM, Gaysorn and club21 / pp group / pacifica brands — no Chiang Mai mall.
