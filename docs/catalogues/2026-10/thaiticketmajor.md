# Thaiticketmajor — October 2026 (ต.ค. 69)

Confidence labels: **verified** (official page read) · **third-party only** · **unverified**. 🏠 = a household card.

## 0. Headline for a Chiang Mai household

- **TTM has no stand-alone outlet in Chiang Mai** (verified). Its 11 main outlets are all in Bangkok and Pathum Thani. In Chiang Mai you can only buy at agent counters: two Major Cineplex cinemas, five Lotus's One Stop Service desks and two Thailand Post offices (§2.2). Paper-ticket pick-up, refunds, upgrades and seat changes go through the 11 main outlets or the show-day booth. So for the household, **buying online with an e-ticket or postal delivery is the practical route**.
- **No event sold on TTM takes place in Chiang Mai** in October–December 2026 (verified absence: 85 listings on `/all-event/` plus the concert, performance, sport, exhibition and TTM LIVE pages). The Chiang Mai events of late November (Yi Peng, Chiang Yai Fest 20+) are sold elsewhere (§8). **TTM LIVE** streaming tickets are the one TTM product the household can use without travelling.
- **Fees that land on the card slip**: a ticket issuance fee of **฿30 per ticket** (฿50 where a plastic souvenir ticket is included), a **3% processing fee** on cards, debit cards, TrueMoney, ShopeePay, Alipay/WeChat and QR PromptPay, and postal delivery of ฿65–80. On some events K Pay Plus and BBL iBanking cost only 0.5%. **None of these fees is refunded** if you return a ticket. (§3)
- **Card and wallet deals that TTM publishes for October** (§6):
  - 🏠 **CardX**: ฿200 cashback on ≥ ฿1,000 per slip, once a month, SMS `CCTM`. Nuta's CardX JCB.
  - 🏠 **KBank**: ฿120 on ≥ ฿200 (฿200 on The Wisdom / The Premier), register in K PLUS on the 1st. Takumi's KBank cards.
  - 🏠 **Central The 1**: ฿100 on ≥ ฿100, register in UCHOOSE on the 1st, 1,500 people a month. Takumi's Central The 1 Redz.
  - **ShopeePay**: ฿300 off orders ≥ ฿5,000.
  - 🏠 **KTC**: redeem points for 0% over 4 months on ≥ ฿3,000, or redeem points equal to the spend for 0% over 4 months plus 10% back.
- **Statement and MCC**: the household's two TTM rows post as `WWW.THAITICKETMAJOR.CO BANGKOK THA` (web) and `THAITICKETMAJOR(TTMEVE BANGKOK THA`. **The MCC wasn't found.** 7922 (ticket agencies) is the standard code for this trade, but it is **unverified** for TTM. (§4)

## 5. TTM's own programmes and deals

### 5.1 TTM CARD (สมาชิก TTM CARD), verified, low value

- Points come from the **service fee only**: **฿30 fee → 150 points**. Ticket prices earn nothing ("ค่าบัตรการแสดงไม่สามารถนำมาสะสมคะแนนได้").
- **5,000 points → ฿100 voucher** for buying tickets. That is ฿0.02 a point, about **฿3 back per ticket**. Points can also go to member-only gifts. Points don't expire.
- Online sign-up is **suspended**: "ขณะนี้อยู่ระหว่างปรับปรุงระบบสมัครสมาชิก TTM CARD ผ่านช่องทางออนไลน์". Join only at the 11 main outlets, all in Bangkok. **Impractical for the household.**
- Sources: [ttmcard](https://www.thaiticketmajor.com/ttmcard/) (points image `bahttopoint.png?v=2025`) and [other.php](https://corporate.thaiticketmajor.com/other.php). The old FAQ said 100 points per ฿20; that is superseded.

### 5.2 Promotion page and other offers (verified)

- `/promotion/` holds **one** item, **"Promotion and Discount REMASTER CONCERT"** (show 10 Oct, Paragon Hall):
  - 15% off with a receipt for 3 packs of ดีน่า UHT, 50 uses, at TTM outlets;
  - 15% off for Carrier "บ้านฉันติดแอร์แคเรียร์" members, from 24 Jul 2569, 20 codes, web only.
  - [promotion](https://www.thaiticketmajor.com/promotion/promotion-and-discount-remaster-concert.html)

- **R CARD**: Muangthai Rachadalai Theatre's membership card (`/rcard/`), which gave a Moulin Rouge pre-sale discount. It is not a TTM programme.

### 5.3 Rules worth knowing

- **Name-on-ticket** (Live Nation shows): the name is printed in English and can't be changed, one name per ticket per show. Resale above face value voids the ticket.
- **Children**: a child ticket depends on height; children over 90 cm pay full price ([about-ticket](https://corporate.thaiticketmajor.com/about-ticket.php)).
