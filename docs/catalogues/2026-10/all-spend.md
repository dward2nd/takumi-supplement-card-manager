## UnionPay Platinum (bbl_up.dom.txt) — verified
- 2% credit, accumulated >= 2,000/month, Thailand or anywhere except China, online+offline, no registration, 1 Jul 69 - 30 Jun 70
- cap 1,000/customer/month (=50,000 spend), 12,000/customer over programme
- principal + supplement combined
- excluded: e-wallet Alipay & WeChat Pay; China; MCC 6211; insurance 5960/6300/6381/6399; cash adv 6010/6011 TC30/31; interest TC11-13; fines 9222; tax 7276/9311; fees 6012/4829; BBL instalments/Be Smart; cancelled; refunds TC41/43; FX purchase; tax refund deducted
- FX converted at posting date
- credited within 60 days after statement ("ภายใน 60 วันหลังจากสรุปยอดบัญชี"), reduces card balance
- 4% on >= 5,000/month in China in CNY (not online), same cap 1,000/month
- points: 1 TYR point per 25 THB (domestic/foreign) — page does not say cashback replaces points
- fee: first-year entrance+annual waived; later years waived with 5,000/yr spend
- Be Smart instalments not available on UnionPay/Amex (credit card page note)
- eligibility (general BBL): age >= 20, income >= 15,000/month (100k Leader/Bumrungrad, 300k M LUXE)
