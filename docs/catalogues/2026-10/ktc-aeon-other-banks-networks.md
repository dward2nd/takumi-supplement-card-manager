## 1. Makro (in store) and Makro PRO

### 1.1 KTC

**A. KTC supermarket points-for-cashback 13% (MKTHM-0549): continuing** · V
- Mechanic: redeem KTC FOREVER points equal to the slip amount (satang rounded up) and get 13% of it back as a credit (0.13 THB a point; the normal value is about 0.10).
- Caps: at most 30,000 points per cardholder per month, across all participating stores. That is ฿3,900 of credit a month.
- Channels:
  - Makro in store: **only** by scanning with the card in KTC Mobile, or by paying through TrueMoney Wallet.
  - Makro PRO online counts.
  - Other listed stores: GO Wholesale (in store and online), Big C, Lotus's, Donki, Golden Place and others.
- Registration: **register every time**, on the day of the spend, at https://ktc.promo/supermarket2026 (or by QR). The confirmation reply is required. Several registered cards are counted per card.
- Credited within 60 days after each calendar month ends.
- Excluded cards: KTC Royal Orchid Plus, KTC Cash Back, KTC Visa Corporate and government cards.
- Period: 1 Aug – 31 Dec 2569.
- Household: all four of Takumi's KTC cards qualify, and so does Baiboon's …2310.
- URL: https://www.ktc.co.th/promotion/shopping/supermarkets-convenience-stores/supermarket

**B. KTC × Makro appliance 0% installments (MOFCE-1073): continuing** · V
- 0% for up to 10 months on participating models, **Makro appliance department only**, on all KTC credit cards and KTC PROUD Mastercard/UnionPay.
- **Installment purchases at Makro earn no KTC points.**
- Period: 1 Jan – 31 Dec 2569. No registration.
- URL: https://www.ktc.co.th/promotion/electronics-mobile/home-appliances/makro-installment
- The older "KTC × Makro" page (0% plus 13% points, SMS `MKP`) ran 20 Jan – 30 Jun 69 and is marked expired: https://www.ktc.co.th/promotion/electronics-mobile/home-appliances/makro

**C. Can KTC cards be swiped at Makro stores?** · V (KTC article, updated 30 Jul 2026)
- KTC's article says: "ถ้าใครไปซื้อของที่ศูนย์จำหน่ายสินค้าแม็คโคร (Makro) ยังไม่สามารถใช้บัตรเครดิต KTC จ่ายเงินได้" (KTC credit cards can't yet be used to pay at Makro stores).
- In the Makro PRO app, KTC Visa, Mastercard, JCB and UnionPay all work.
- That agrees with the 13% terms, where in-store Makro counts only through KTC Mobile scan or TrueMoney.
- URL: https://www.ktc.co.th/article/shopping/shop-online/makro-credit-card-acceptance
- No other KTC × Makro or KTC × Makro PRO campaign (code or cashback) is in KTC's promotion sitemap. The only Makro pages are `makro` (expired) and `makro-installment`.

### 1.3 Bangkok Bank (BBL)

**BSUP "ช็อปซูเปอร์ฯคุ้ม" points-for-cashback: continuing, ends 31 Oct** · V
- Mechanic: for every ฿1,000 on a slip (minimum ฿1,000), redeem 1,000 Thank You Rewards points.
  - Most BBL cards get ฿130 back (13%).
  - Pinnacle, Infinite and ผู้นำแพลทินัม (Platinum leader) cards get ฿150 (15%).
- Stores: **Makro and Makro PRO (www.makro.pro)**, Big C (plus online), Donki, Dear Tummy, Gourmet, Foodland, Lotus's (plus online), MaxValu, Rimping, Tops and Villa. **GO Wholesale is not listed.** The page doesn't say whether a Makro in-store payment must be a card swipe or a QR scan.
- Caps: at most 20,000 points per person per month, all cards together (฿2,600–3,000).
- Eligible cards: full payment only, **primary cards only**. Excluded: Titanium, AirAsia, Bangkok Bank M Visa and supplements.
- Registration: SMS `BSUP <16-digit card number>` to 4712008 (฿3) **on the day of purchase**.
- Credited within 90 days.
- Period: 1 Aug – 31 Oct 2569.
- URL: https://www.bangkokbank.com/th-TH/Personal/Cards/Credit-Cards/Promotions/Shop_Super_Koom_260801-261031
- The site blocks curl; the page was read in headless Chrome.
