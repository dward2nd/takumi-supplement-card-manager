## 2. Buying from Apple itself

### 2.1 Where

- **Apple Store online** `apple.com/th/shop` and the **Apple Store app** (on the Thai App Store, seller Apple Distribution International) · verified. Delivery is free on everything ("ไม่มีค่าธรรมเนียมการจัดส่งสินค้าทุกประเภท"); returns 14 days, free, by mail (ld+json `merchantReturnDays: 14`). Phone orders **1800-01-9209**.
- **Apple retail stores in Thailand: two, both in Bangkok** · verified ([storelist](https://www.apple.com/th/retail/storelist/)):
  - **Apple Central World**, 4, 4/1-4/2, 4/4 ถนนราชดำริห์, ปทุมวัน, 1800 019 448
  - **Apple Iconsiam**, 299 ถนนเจริญนคร, คลองสาน, 1800 019 447
  - **None in Chiang Mai.** In Chiang Mai the nearest Apple-run option is the online store with delivery; the nearest "Apple Premium Partner" is Studio7 at Central Festival Chiangmai (§5).
- The seller of record for Apple Store gift cards is **บริษัท แอปเปิ้ล เซาท์ เอเชีย (ประเทศไทย) จำกัด** (Apple South Asia (Thailand) Ltd.) ([gift cards](https://www.apple.com/th/shop/gift-cards)) · verified.

### 2.2 Payment (verified, [help/payments](https://www.apple.com/th/shop/help/payments))

- **Credit cards accepted online: Mastercard, Visa, American Express only.** JCB and UnionPay are **not listed** — so 🏠 JCB and UnionPay cards (e.g. Nuta's CardX JCB and AEON UnionPay, Baiboon's KTC UnionPay) can't pay at apple.com/th by this page. Whether the two Bangkok stores take JCB/UnionPay at the till: not found.
- Other methods: ATM bank transfer with a pay-in slip (SCB, Krungthai, Bangkok Bank; expires in 3 days), over-the-counter payment (Big C, TrueMoney, Pay@Post, Lotus's; **orders ≤ ฿30,000 only**), Apple Store gift cards (up to 8 per order, plus one credit/debit card).
- Cards issued with billing addresses in the US, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia and the Philippines are accepted. "ธนาคารพาณิชย์ในประเทศไทยจะเป็นผู้ดำเนินการชำระเงิน … ไม่ควรมีค่าธรรมเนียมจากธนาคารเพิ่มเติม": a Thai bank acquires the payment, so an apple.com/th order should post as a **domestic** charge (unlike `APPLE.COM/BILL … IRL` App Store rows, §6).
- **When the card is charged**: full payment is charged **when the item ships** (the amount is only authorised at order); **installment orders are charged the evening of the order date**. Split shipments can post as several charges.

### 2.3 0% installments at Apple (verified, [finance/installment](https://www.apple.com/th/shop/browse/finance/installment))

- **0% for 3, 6 or 10 months** with **CardX, SCB, KBank, UOB, Krungsri Consumer group cards ("บัตรเครดิตในเครือกรุงศรีคอนซูมเมอร์"), ttb and Bangkok Bank**.
- Minimum: the purchase must be **over ฿5,000**. Consumer credit cards only; **American Express and debit cards are excluded**. Subject to the issuer's approval.
- **KTC and AEON are not on Apple's list** · verified (absence).
- The terms footer says "ข้อมูลนี้เป็นข้อมูลล่าสุด ณ วันที่ 31 ธันวาคม 2025".

The two figures weren't reconciled; per-model values need the device's IMEI/serial at the partner site.

Devices without value are recycled free.

### 2.6 AppleCare+, offers and gift cards (verified)

- **AppleCare+** (full payment, from the buy pages' `APPLECARE_BOOTSTRAP`): iPhone 18 Pro / Pro Max / Air **฿8,590**, iPhone 17 ฿6,590, iPhone 17e ฿4,990, MacBook Air from ฿7,590 (฿8,590 for the other size), iPad Air 11" ฿3,190 / 13" ฿3,890, Apple Watch Ultra 4 ฿3,790. AppleCare One (several devices on one plan) is also offered (newsroom).
- **Apple's own offers** ([apple.com/th/promo](https://www.apple.com/th/promo/)): new subscribers get **Apple Music free for 3 months** with a new iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Apple Watch, Mac, AirPods, HomePod or Beats, and **Apple Creator Studio free for 3 months** with a new Mac or iPad. No price discount and no bank offer is published on apple.com/th.
- **Apple Store gift cards** are **email-only** in any amount shown on the page, never expire, can be used **only at apple.com/th or by phone** (and per the payments page at the Bangkok stores), and **not at resellers**. They are not a discount route.
