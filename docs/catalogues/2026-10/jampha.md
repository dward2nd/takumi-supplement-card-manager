## Summary for the page

- **Jampha is แจ่มฟ้า, not จำปา.** "Jampha" is the company's own spelling of `แจ่มฟ้า` (bright sky). The company is **`บริษัท แจ่มฟ้า เซฟมาร์ท จำกัด` / JAMPHA SAVEMART COMPANY LIMITED**. It is a family-owned local supermarket chain from Lamphun that sells both retail and wholesale. It has **25 VAT-registered outlets: 18 in Chiang Mai and 7 in Lamphun**. Its statement descriptor (`JAMPHA SAVEMART CO.,LTD. CHIANGMAI TH`) doesn't say which branch.
- **How to pay (per the household, user 2026-10-06)**:
  - **UnionPay QR is the only credit-card QR it takes.** Bank-app credit-card QR (Krungsri UCHOOSE, KTC, UOB TMRW, K PLUS) doesn't work.
  - **PromptPay / Thai QR from bank accounts** works.
  - **A physical card (tap or insert) costs 2% extra.**
  - **Online sources neither confirm nor contradict the QR rule or the 2% fee.** The household's own ledger supports both (§2.2).
- **Wallets**: the LINE OA lists **LINE Pay**. **TrueMoney, ShopeePay and Alipay+ were not found** anywhere.
- **One bank campaign names Jampha. KTC `JFM`** (1 Mar – 31 Dec 2026, every branch): redeem KTC FOREVER points equal to the slip amount for **10% cashback** (฿0.10 a point). Register **on the day, for each slip**, by SMS `JFM <16-digit card>#<slip amount>` to 0613845000. There's no cap. **Baiboon's KTC UnionPay qualifies.** Whether a UnionPay **QR** payment counts is **unverified**.
- **Own promotions**:
  - `แจ่มฟ้า โปรแจ่ม!! ทุกสาขา`, **25 Sep – 10 Oct 2569**: shelf-tag price cuts, buy-1-get-1 and multi-buy deals.
  - Supplier events: สหพัฒน์แฟร์ (1–4 Oct 2569) and Singha Water's Tesla draw (1 Oct – 31 Dec 2569).
  - **No member card or points scheme was found.** Nothing is announced for November yet.
- **Government**: it takes the **บัตรสวัสดิการแห่งรัฐ** (state welfare card) at every branch in October. **The 60/40 (`ไทยช่วยไทย พลัส (เพิ่มเติม)`) almost certainly doesn't apply**, because Jampha is a large juristic company.
- **Logo**: the colour horizontal lockup is at `https://jampha.co.th/_nuxt/logo.Bys84TXb.png`. It has a blue (`#168ECD`) icon with orange (`#EF7A26`) arcs, then `แจ่มฟ้า` in blue, `เซฟมาร์ท` in orange and `JAMPHA SAVEMART` in black.

## 6. For the spending plan (reading, not research)

- **Pay by UnionPay QR on KTC UnionPay, never by tapping a card.**
  - The tap costs 2%, more than almost any card reward.
  - The QR takes 6% off, at most ฿60 a slip, **one slip per card number per day**, ฿300 per card a month, while UnionPay's monthly pool lasts. September's pool ran out on the 12th, so **go early in the month**.
- **On a big visit, the ฿60 cap is what bites.** It is 0.75% of ฿8,000 and 0.2% of ฿30,000.
  - Splitting the bill only helps **across card numbers**: one slip on Baiboon's …2310 and one on Takumi's …1346.
  - A third slip on either card the same day gets nothing off.
- **Add KTC `JFM` if there are KTC points to spend.** Text `JFM <card>#<amount>` to 0613845000 the same day, for each slip, for 10% back at ฿0.10 a point, with no cap. Whether the QR slip counts is the open question (§5.1).
- **PromptPay from a bank account** costs nothing extra and earns nothing. It is the fallback when the UnionPay pool is empty.
- **Avoid First Choice at Jampha** while the 2% fee applies, NW4 included.

## Site tricks

New ones, not yet in [[../research-methods]]:
- **dataforthai.com is behind Cloudflare** (curl, WebFetch and `r.jina.ai` get 403 or a challenge). **Headless Chrome passes it**:
  - `/company/<tax id>/` gives the DBD registration;
  - `/company-branch/<tax id>/` gives **every VAT-registered branch with its address**. That is the best branch list for a chain whose own pages don't keep one.
- **Facebook pages through `r.jina.ai`**:
  - `https://r.jina.ai/https://www.facebook.com/<page>` returns the cover image, intro, contact fields and the latest ~10 photos without a login. Adding `/about_details` gives the long description and the latest ~10 reels with view counts. `https://r.jina.ai/https://www.facebook.com/reel/<id>` gives a reel's full caption.
  - **fbcdn thumbnails enlarge** if `ctp=s160x160` is changed to `ctp=s960x960` in the URL. The signature still holds.
- **Google Maps in headless Chrome** (`/maps/search/<query>?hl=th`, `--virtual-time-budget=25000`) works in "limited view":
  - each search lists about 5 places with name, house number, phone and that day's closing time;
  - a query that resolves to one place gives its address and the day's hours in `aria-label`s;
  - **run one search per district, in parallel** (a separate `--user-data-dir` for each) to cover a chain.
- **Find a merchant's bank page by searching for the merchant's name restricted to the banks' domains.** The KTC `JFM` page didn't show up in general searches for the merchant. A web search with `allowed_domains` set to the bank sites found it at once.
- **KTC promotion pages carry their full terms in the raw HTML**, inside Next.js `self.__next_f.push` chunks, so `curl` plus a grep for the SMS code is enough. Headless Chrome also renders them.
- **ONE.th "NexGen Commerce" shops** (jampha.co.th) are Nuxt apps. The `/_nuxt/*.js` bundles show the platform's checkout options (`qrcode`, card, `SPayLater`, COD), but not which ones the shop has switched on.
