## The payment, as CMU takes it ([[cmu-tuition]], verified there)

- **When**: `วันชำระค่าธรรมเนียมการศึกษา` **30 Nov – 4 Dec 2026** (late, with a fine, 7–11 Dec). So the slip is either a **November** spend (30 Nov) or a **December** spend (1–4 Dec) for every monthly campaign, and falls in each card's cycle that opens in late November.
- **Channels and fees**: the **Thai QR in the registration system is the only fee-free one**; credit card **online 2.1%** (added into one total); credit card at the **Finance Division counter (`กองคลัง`) 0.9%** (until 16:30); Alipay / WeChat Pay 1.7%; cash at bank counters with the `มชท.50` slip. Paying the slip's barcode from a bank app isn't allowed.
- **Fees on the three sizes**: counter ฿540 / ฿720 / ฿900 and online ฿1,260 / ฿1,680 / ฿2,100 on ฿60,000 / ฿80,000 / ฿100,000. An offer is only worth using on a card channel if it pays more than that.
- **MCC: unknown** (8220 is a reading, [[cmu-tuition]] §5). Every row below says whether it survives **8220** (colleges and universities) and **9399** (government services).
- **Networks**: CMU names none; whether JCB, UnionPay or Amex work at either card channel is unknown.

**Baiboon's cards are supplements on Takumi's accounts** ([[../../people/baiboon]]), so every cap and ladder that is "per primary account" is shared with Takumi's (and Nuta's, where she has a supplement on the same account) spend that month. For welcome offers it also means she already "holds" a card at every issuer in her list.

**What the household had used by 22 Oct** (the Promotion Bureau's `write-catalogue status` snapshot in the session scratchpad): NW4 October pool ฿14,261.88 → ฿200; UOB One 1% cycle 22 Oct – 24 Nov ฿0 of ฿2,000; ttb so smart 1% cycle 27 Sep – 26 Oct ฿35.04 of ฿2,000. The cycles that will hold the tuition (UOB One from 25 Nov, ttb from 27 Nov) haven't started. September's pooled NW3 spend was ฿36,803.92 ([[../../promotions/first-choice-nw3]]), a fair guess at a month's other pooled spend.

**Household precedent for a university slip**: Takumi's `CHULALONGKORN UNIVERSITY BANGKOK` ฿33,500 (31 Jul 2026, KBank PLUSTINUM) carries 1,340 points realised (1 per ฿25) and no promotion link · household. Chula waives its card fee for KBank cards ([[cmu-tuition]] §1.4); CMU doesn't. No CMU tuition row exists in any of the three ledgers (searched `UNIVERSITY`, `CHIANG MAI UNI`, `CMU`, `มหาวิทยาลัย`, `ค่าเทอม`, `TUITION`).

**Reading the table.**
- **On the card channel the household has three real options**: First Choice (NW4), Lotus's Beyond (only if invited) and Central The 1 REDZ (`ED1`). Use them **at the counter**. Online, the 2.1% wipes out everything except NW4 and the invited Lotus's ladders.
- **NW4 and the cap**: NW4 pays ฿200 per whole ฿10,000 of the month's pooled First Choice spend (all three holders), up to ฿2,000 a month. With a typical ≈ ฿35,000 of other pooled spend in the month, the tuition adds ฿1,200 on ฿60,000 but only ฿1,400 on ฿80,000 or ฿100,000. The cap takes the rest. Paying on **30 Nov** puts it in November's pool, on **1–4 Dec** in December's; pick the month with less other First Choice spend. The campaign cap is ฿6,000 per primary account for Oct–Dec.
- **One slip, one card**: these offers sit on different cards, so they don't stack on one tuition payment. Choose one card.
