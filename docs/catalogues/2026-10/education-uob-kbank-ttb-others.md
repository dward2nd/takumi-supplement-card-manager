# University tuition — UOB, KBank, ttb, SPayLater and other banks — October 2026 (ต.ค. 69)

Siblings: [[cmu-tuition]] (CMU's channels, fees and dates) · [[education-krungsri-ktc-aeon]] (Krungsri group, KTC, AEON) · [[all-spend-tuition]] (offers that name no merchant, wallets such as TrueMoney and SPayLater, welcome offers).

🏠 = a card the household holds. Baiboon's cards in scope are all on Takumi's accounts: **UOB One** (Mastercard Signature, supplement), **UOB World** (Mastercard, supplement), **UOB Premier** (Mastercard, supplement …6040), **UOB Makro** (Visa; she spends on Takumi's …1649), **KBank PLUSTINUM** (Visa; Takumi's …3831, no supplement section), **KBank JCB** (Takumi's …2511), **ttb so smart** (Visa, supplement …0864), **SPayLater**. Card notes: [[../../cards/uob-one]] · [[../../cards/uob-world]] · [[../../cards/uob-makro]] · [[../../cards/kbank-plustinum]] · [[../../cards/ttb-so-smart]].

**Confidence labels**: **verified** (the issuer's own page, read today) · **household** (the household's ledger, Bureau or statements show it) · **third-party only** · **unverified** (inference or not found). A **reading** is an inference from the terms, labelled as such.

## The payment ([[cmu-tuition]], verified there)

- **When**: **30 Nov – 4 Dec 2026** (late, with a fine, 7–11 Dec). 30 Nov is a November spend; 1–4 Dec are December spend for every monthly campaign.
- **Channels**: the **Thai QR in CMU's registration system is the only fee-free one**. A credit card costs **2.1% online** (added into one total) or **0.9% at the Finance Division counter** (`กองคลัง`, until 16:30). Alipay / WeChat Pay 1.7%; cash at bank counters with the `มชท.50` slip. No CMU installment plan; **no bank's cards are exempt from the fee**. SCB issues CMU's student ID cards and the payment system sits under `scbqr`, so SCB is the likely acquirer.
- **Fees**: counter ฿540 / ฿720 / ฿900 and online ฿1,260 / ฿1,680 / ฿2,100 on ฿60,000 / ฿80,000 / ฿100,000.
- **Rewards on the fee**: every card below is rated on the whole charge (tuition + fee, 100.9% or 102.1%), assuming the fee posts in the same slip as the tuition. CMU's online page adds it into one total; whether the counter puts the 0.9% on the same slip isn't published. No issuer in scope excludes a merchant's surcharge: KBank's `ค่าธรรมเนียมทุกประเภท` and UOB's `ค่าธรรมเนียมทุกประเภท` list the bank's own fees (annual, late, cash-advance), not a merchant's (reading).
- **MCC**: unknown. **8220** (colleges and universities) is a reading ([[cmu-tuition]] §5). Each section says what changes under **8299** (schools, n.e.c.) or **9399** (government services).
- **Household precedent**: Takumi's `CHULALONGKORN UNIVERSITY BANGKOK` ฿33,500 (31 Jul 2026, KBank PLUSTINUM, bill cycle 25 Aug) carries 1,340 K Point realised (= ฿33,500 ÷ 25), the ordinary rate · household. **Chula waives its card fee for KBank cards** (user, 2026-10-22); CMU waives it for nobody. Baiboon's ledger has no tuition row (only CMU canteen, fitness and petrol rows).

**Point values used** (as in [[all-spend-hospital-spa]]): UOB Rewards ฿0.10 (≈ ฿0.104 through The 1; UOB Makro points ฿0.111 when burnt at Makro, `9 คะแนน = 1 บาท`) · K Point ฿0.10 at a card terminal or through KBank's 10% burns (`ED`), ฿0.083 as a K PLUS cash rebate.

## 1. At a glance

### 1.1 Fee waivers: none for CMU

- **No issuer in scope waives or absorbs CMU's card fee**, and none lists CMU among the institutions where it does. · verified (each programme's list read) / not found (CMU)
- What exists elsewhere:
  - **CardX** "เปิดเทอมใหม่ … **ฟรีค่าธรรมเนียมในการรูดบัตรเครดิต**" (1 Apr 69 – 31 Mar 70): a free card fee at participating schools and universities, full pay or installment. The banners show only Bangkok institutions (Mahidol's College of Management and College of Music, Bangkok University, Rangsit, Sripatum, SIIT, international schools). Its list pages now return 404, so the full list couldn't be read (§6.1).
  - **UOB IPW887**: `ไม่มีค่าธรรมเนียมการใช้บัตร` at 54 international schools and SIIT, on UOB's own terminals (§2.1).
  - **KBank at Chula**: the user's precedent. No KBank page announces a university fee waiver (§3.1).
- So at CMU **every card pays 0.9% or 2.1%**, and a card only beats the free Thai QR when its reward on the slip tops the fee.

- **How to weigh them** (user's framing): a 0% plan's cost is CMU's fee plus the card reward it gives up, against the cash-flow benefit.
