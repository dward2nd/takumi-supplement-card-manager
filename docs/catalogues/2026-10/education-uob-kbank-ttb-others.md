Baiboon's cards in scope (supplements on Takumi's accounts): **UOB One** (Mastercard Signature), **UOB World** (Mastercard), **UOB Premier** (Mastercard), **UOB Makro** (Visa), **KBank PLUSTINUM** (Visa), **KBank JCB**, **ttb so smart** (Visa), **SPayLater**. **Confidence labels**: **verified** (the issuer's own page, read today) · **household** (the household's ledger or statements show it) · **third-party only** · **unverified** (inference or not found).

**Household evidence.** Takumi paid `CHULALONGKORN UNIVERSITY BANGKOK` ฿33,500 on KBank PLUSTINUM on 31 Jul 2026 (bill cycle 25 Aug). The ledger row earns 1,340 K Point, which is ฿33,500 ÷ 25: the ordinary rate, so that university slip wasn't excluded from K Point. Baiboon's ledger has no earlier tuition row (only CMU canteen, fitness and petrol rows).

**Point values used** (as in [[all-spend-hospital-spa]]): UOB Rewards ≈ ฿0.10 (≈ ฿0.104 through The 1; UOB Makro points ฿0.111 when burnt at Makro, `9 คะแนน = 1 บาท`) · K Point ฿0.10 at a card terminal, ฿0.083 through K PLUS.

**MCC.** A Thai university normally posts as **MCC 8220** (colleges and universities). A state university's payment page can also post as **9399** (government services) or **8299** (schools and educational services, n.e.c.). None of the household's statements shows CMU's code; the Chulalongkorn row shows only that 8220-type spend earned K Point. Each card section says what changes under 9399 or 8299.

### 2.2 Paying tuition into a bank account — UOB PayAnything (principal cards only) · verified

- **What it is**: `UOB PayAnything` transfers money from the card's credit line to any bank account, `เช่น ค่าเช่าบ้าน ค่าเทอม` ("such as rent, tuition"), with `ค่าธรรมเนียมในอัตราไม่เกิน 2.0%` (a fee of up to 2%, VAT included), per transfer ≤ ฿699,999. Online through UOB LINE Connect or the UOB website. [PayAnything promotion page](https://www.uob.co.th/personal/credit-cards/promotions/shopping-lifestyle/payanything-promotion.page) (headless).
- **Who**: `ผู้ถือบัตรเครดิตยูโอบี และบัตรหลักทุกประเภท` — **principal cards**. Baiboon's supplements can't use it; Takumi's principal could.
- **Earning**: it counts as card spend for points or cashback, `ไม่เกิน 100,000 บาท/บัญชี/รอบบัญชี`. UOB One pays 1% on it (`สำหรับโปรแกรม UOB PayAnything จะได้รับเครดิตเงินคืน 1% รวมสูงสุดไม่เกิน 100,000 บาทต่อรอบบัญชี`); UOB World ×2; UOB Premier's ×2 with the same ฿100,000 cap; UOB Makro excludes PayAnything above ฿100,000.
- **Q4 promotion `UPA`** (1 Oct – 31 Dec 69, ref `26PA77`): register **every month** (SMS `UPA <last 12 digits>` → 4545111, or Rewards+); cashback by the month's transfers on one card: ฿110,000–219,999 → ฿100 (own-name account) / ฿100 (someone else's) / **฿200 (a juristic-person account, e.g. a university)**; ฿220,000–399,999 → ฿200 / 300 / 400; ฿400,000–599,999 → ฿1,000 / 1,500 / 2,000; ≥ ฿600,000 → ฿2,000 / 2,500 / 4,000. Credited by 31 Mar 2570. First-time users who move ≥ ฿100,000 between 1 Oct and 30 Nov also get a Snoopy clutch (฿280).
- **Verdict**: a fee of up to 2% against 1% back (UOB One) or ≈ 0.8% (World ×2). **It costs money** unless the fee offered is under the card's rate; a ฿60,000–100,000 tuition doesn't reach the ฿110,000 bonus floor. Useful only if CMU won't take a card and the household must pay by transfer.

### 2.3 Turning a full-pay slip into installments — UOB PayLater · verified

[UOB PayLater](https://www.uob.co.th/personal/cards/credit/uob-paylater.page): any not-yet-billed card slip from ฿1,500, up to 36 months, **10.98%–16% a year** on credit cards; through UOB TMRW, LINE Connect or the call centre. No 0% for tuition. UOB re-pays the slip's points or cashback per term instead ([[../../promotions/uob-one-2026]] *Installment rule*).

### 2.4 Each UOB card at a tuition slip (MCC 8220)

**No UOB exclusion list names education, MCC 8220/8299, or government services (9399).** Read on 22 Oct on each card page (headless render).

- **UOB One 🏠 — 1%** · verified. [Card page](https://www.uob.co.th/personal/credit-cards/cash-back/one-cash-back-credit-card.page).
  - The 1% excludes funds, unit-linked, not-yet-billed installments, cash advance, fuel, Makro, electricity and water bills, Fund Transfer, FX, interest, fines, fees, cancellations, business use, foreign-THB, MCC 4900 and e-wallet top-ups (from 1 Jan 68), and IPW887's school installments. **Nothing about education.**
  - Cap ฿2,000 a statement cycle, principal + supplements pooled (`ยอดใช้จ่ายบัตรเสริมจะถูกคำนวณรวมเข้ากับยอดใช้จ่ายบัตรหลัก`); spend posted on the statement date counts in the next cycle.
  - The 10% / 5% tiers are named merchants only (BTS via LINE Pay / Rabbit Rewards, MRT, Café Amazon; 7-Eleven, Grab, Watsons). Tuition is in the 1% tier.
  - Household: the cycle that opened 22 Oct (to 24 Nov) had ฿0 of 1% spend on 22 Oct, so the full ฿2,000 is free; the October cycle (25 Sep – 21 Oct) used ฿294.91 of it (Bureau rows `2026M11 — UOB One cb 1%`, `2026M10 — UOB One cb 1%`).
  - **฿60,000 → ฿600; ฿100,000 → ฿1,000 (1.0%).** Under 9399 or 8299: the same (no government or education carve-out).
- **UOB World 🏠 — ×2, or ×5 if CMU's payment page is an e-commerce transaction** · verified. [Card page](https://www.uob.co.th/personal/credit-cards/rewards/uob-world-credit-card.page).
  - Base `2 เท่า ทุกการใช้จ่าย 25 บาท สำหรับยอดการใช้จ่ายอื่นๆ` = 2 points per ฿25 ≈ **0.8%**.
  - ×5 (5 points per ฿25 ≈ 2%) covers `การใช้จ่ายออนไลน์, e-wallet, หมวดร้านอาหาร, หมวดท่องเที่ยว, และเงินสกุลต่างประเทศ รวมสูงสุด 20,000 บาทต่อรอบบัญชี`, and the online leg is `เฉพาะรายการที่ถูกกำหนดให้เป็นรายการ e-Commerce โดยมาสเตอร์การ์ดและวีซ่าเท่านั้น`. A tuition paid on a university's **online payment page** carries the network's e-commerce flag, so its first ฿20,000 (of the account's cycle quota, shared with Takumi) would earn ×5 (reading; no household tuition row to confirm).
  - Exclusions as UOB One's (no education). Points go to the principal's account.
  - Household: the cycle that opened 22 Oct had ฿0 of the ฿20,000 used; the cycle to 21 Oct used ฿19,006.25 (Bureau rows `2026M11 — UOB World ×5`, `2026M10 — UOB World ×5`).
  - **In person (EDC)**: ฿60,000 → 4,800 points (≈ ฿480–500, 0.8%); ฿100,000 → 8,000 (≈ ฿800–830).
  - **Online, quota unused**: ฿60,000 → 4,000 + 3,200 = 7,200 points (≈ ฿720–750, **1.2%**); ฿100,000 → 4,000 + 6,400 = 10,400 (≈ ฿1,040–1,080, **1.04%**). Every baht of quota already used in the cycle moves the rate toward 0.8%.
- **UOB Premier 🏠 — ×2 domestic** · verified. [Card page](https://www.uob.co.th/personal/credit-cards/shopping/uob-premier-credit-card.page). `รับคะแนนสะสมยูโอบี รีวอร์ด พลัส 2 เท่าทุกการใช้จ่ายในประเทศ`; ×4 only at MCC 5311/5309. Exclusions as above (no education). **≈ 0.8%**: ฿60,000 → 4,800 points; ฿100,000 → 8,000. Its other offers don't fit: `PRE` 0% × 3 is fashion MCCs only; the Miracle Lounge 2027 right (≥ ฿50,000 on the card 1 Aug – 31 Dec 69) is the principal's (`สงวนสิทธิ์เฉพาะผู้ถือบัตรเครดิตยูโอบี พรีเมียร์ … โดยไม่สามารถโอนสิทธิ์ให้ผู้ติดตาม`; whether supplement spend counts toward it isn't stated). Household note: `scripts/repositories/cards/uob-premier.yaml` records Baiboon's supplement (…6040) as active only to Jan 2026, so check the card still works.
- **UOB Makro 🏠 (Baiboon spends on Takumi's …1649) — 1 point per ฿25, + UMK26 mission 3** · verified. [Card page](https://www.uob.co.th/personal/credit-cards/rewards/uob-makro-rewards-credit-card.page), [MPW823](https://www.uob.co.th/personal/credit-cards/promotions/shopping-lifestyle/makro-26th-mpw823-1226.page).
  - `ยอดใช้จ่ายทุก 25 บาท ที่แม็คโคร โปร และการใช้จ่ายอื่นๆ` = 1 point; ×2 on the 16th of the month (SMS `MMID` once; whether Takumi registered it isn't recorded). ≤ 150,000 points per account per calendar year.
  - Exclusions: cancellations, cash advance, transfers, fuel, funds (MCC 6211), PayAnything above ฿100,000, fees, interest, FX, business use, foreign-THB, utilities and e-wallet top-ups. **No education.**
  - **UMK26 mission 3** (`ภารกิจที่ 3`): ≥ ฿10,000 a calendar month of "other" spend (not Makro, not MCC 5411, not e-wallets, PayAnything, fuel, installments …; no education exclusion) → **฿300**, ≤ ฿900 per cardholder (principal + supplements) over Oct–Dec; it is also one of the three missions for the ฿1,000 Makro voucher. Every ฿1,000 is a draw right (gold bars, Makro vouchers). Registered (`UMK26`, Takumi, 3 Oct). The Bureau row `2026M10 — UMK26-3 cb ฿300` read ฿0 of qualifying spend on 22 Oct, so a tuition slip would earn the month's ฿300 unless other spend gets there first.
  - **฿60,000 → 2,400 points (≈ ฿240–267) + ฿300 = ≈ ฿540–570 (≈ 0.9%)**; on the 16th with `MMID`: 4,800 points (≈ ฿480–533) + ฿300 ≈ 1.3–1.4%. **฿100,000 → 4,000 points (≈ ฿400–444) + ฿300 ≈ 0.7%**; on the 16th ≈ 1.1–1.2%. The ฿300 is fixed, so it's worth less per baht on a bigger slip.
- **SPW796** (online / e-wallet ฿100 per ฿5,000, ≤ ฿200 a month) counts named apps only ([[../../promotions/uob-epw538]]): a tuition payment page isn't one. ✗.
- **No UOB points-for-tuition burn** exists: the points-to-cashback pages are hospitals (`UH`/`HPW116`), insurance (`IPW112`), Makro, Thai Airways and the generic Pay with Points (no education category, no published rate) · verified (absence in the 648-item list).
